Arthitean Cranial Subspace and Brain-Lobe Architecture

Arthitean Cranial Subspace is the maximumlessly large internal subspace contained within an Arthitean skull in which the Arthitean brain exists despite the skull retaining ordinary external dimensions.

Arthitean Brain Neural Amount is the maximumless amount of neurons contained within an Arthitean brain.

Despite Arthitean Cranial Subspace and Arthitean Brain Neural Amount, an Arthitean's head and brain do not feel any heavier or lighter than they ordinarily do to that Arthitean.

Instant Neural Transfer is the property whereby signal transfer between any two Arthitean neurons is, at its slowest, instantaneous regardless of the internal subspatial distance separating them.

Brain-lobe is a primary neural structure specialized for a distinct mental function. In Arthitean neurobiology, a mental function is not limited to ordinary cognition: it includes any distinct function that can be performed, produced, regulated, defended, resisted, transformed, generated, simulated, preserved, inhibited, amplified, or otherwise implemented by an Arthitean mind or neural system.

Brain-lobe Function Scope Clarification: 'Mental function' must be interpreted maximally broadly for Arthiteans. It does not mean only reasoning, memory, attention, language, planning, emotion, perception, or other familiar cognitive faculties. If a distinct operation can meaningfully be performed by a mind or neural system, then it can in principle be the specialization of a Brain-lobe. The function may be cognitive, metacognitive, defensive, resistant, adversarial, regulatory, self-preserving, self-modifying, power-controlling, perception-altering, identity-preserving, anti-manipulation, anti-hax, temporal, conceptual, or something for which ordinary human neurobiology has no analogue at all.

Function Type Is Open-Ended: There is no closed catalogue of allowable Brain-lobe functions. Arthitean Brain-lobe architecture is function-driven rather than human-anatomy-driven. A lobe is not disqualified merely because its specialization would sound impossible, supernatural, excessively narrow, or extremely powerful by ordinary standards. The relevant question is simply whether the function can exist as a mental or neural function for an Arthitean. If yes, it is eligible for lobar specialization.

Function Kind and Function Extent Are Separate: The name of a Brain-lobe specifies what kind of function it performs; the Brain-lobe Factors and the Arthitean's wider physiology determine the extent, speed, power, efficiency, selectivity, plasticity, specialization, and other performance properties of that function. Therefore, a Mind-Control-Resistance Brain-lobe is not restricted to ordinary psychological resistance. Its functional extent can scale through Brain-lobe Factors One through Twenty-Two and the PHK system just as other Arthitean biological functions scale.

Example - Mind-Control Resistance: A dedicated Brain-lobe may have the function of resisting mind control itself. Its neural activity can detect foreign executive commands, distinguish self-generated intention from imposed intention, suppress hostile control pathways, preserve autonomous choice, and adapt to previously successful control methods. Recursive subBrain-lobes could separately specialize in intrusion detection, command isolation, executive self-authentication, hostile-signal inhibition, and adaptive counter-resistance.

Example - Identity Preservation: A Brain-lobe may specialize in preserving the Arthitean's identity against personality rewriting, memory-based identity alteration, possession, imposed personas, consciousness fragmentation, or other attempts to change who is making decisions. The existence of such a function is not metaphorical: identity preservation can be the primary job of a physical Arthitean neural structure.

Example - Cognitohazard and Memetic Defense: A Brain-lobe may specialize in cognitohazard neutralization, memetic-contamination resistance, or safe comprehension of mentally dangerous information. Such a lobe could quarantine dangerous representations, transform them into harmless internal encodings, prevent automatic propagation into other Brain-lobes, and permit useful analysis without allowing the hazardous mental effect to operate normally.

Example - Thought-Reading Counterintelligence: A Brain-lobe may specialize in resisting telepathic extraction, obscuring sensitive thoughts, generating decoy mental content, authenticating incoming telepathic signals, or selectively exposing only chosen information. This is still a mental function even though its purpose is defensive and adversarial rather than ordinary cognition.

Example - Impossible or Alien Cognition: A Brain-lobe may specialize in comprehending paradoxical, alien, conceptually impossible, higher-ontological, maximumless, or otherwise non-ordinary structures. Another lobe could specialize in emulating an alien mode of thought without allowing that temporary cognitive architecture to destabilize the Arthitean's ordinary identity or other functions.

Example - Power and Hax Control: A Brain-lobe may specialize in mentally controlling a power, coordinating many powers simultaneously, distinguishing hostile effects from harmless effects, managing hax interactions, or controlling metaeffects that operate on other effects. If precise neural control of a supernatural capability is a mental function, then that control function can possess dedicated lobar architecture.

Example - Temporal and Distributed-Self Cognition: A Brain-lobe may specialize in preserving usable memory through time loops, discriminating between memories from different timelines, integrating information from distributed avatars or copies of the self, or coordinating multiple concurrent streams of consciousness. None of these functions need resemble a conventional human cognitive faculty in order to qualify.

Example - Maximumless Neural Coordination: Because Arthitean Brain Neural Amount is maximumless and Instant Neural Transfer removes ordinary distance delay, specialized lobes can exist for neural addressing, maximumless parallelism management, maximumless attention allocation, maximumless crossfunctionality management, or coordination of maximumlessly many simultaneous neural operations. Such functions may themselves require further recursive sublobar specialization.

SubBrain-lobe is a neural subdivision of a Brain-lobe specialized for a subfunction of that Brain-lobe's function.

Recursive Sublobar Specialization is the organization of subBrain-lobes into specialized subsubBrain-lobes and recursively deeper subdivisions that perform progressively more specific subfunctions of their parent function.

Brain-lobe Generation is the generation of a new Brain-lobe when sufficient demand exists for a mental function that cannot be adequately produced by any existing Brain-lobe function or interBrain-lobe crossfunctionality.

Brain-lobe Generation Scope: Brain-lobe Generation therefore responds to the full open-ended space of Arthitean mental functions, not merely to shortages in ordinary cognition. If existing Brain-lobes and their crossfunctionality cannot adequately produce a demanded mental function, the brain can generate a new primary Brain-lobe whose reason for existing is that function.

Generation Example - Escalating Mind Control: Suppose an opponent uses a form of mind control that existing inhibition, identity, and metaneural systems cannot adequately resist. The unmet demand can justify generation of a dedicated Mind-Control-Resistance Brain-lobe. Continued attacks can then drive subBrain-lobes for detecting that control method, resisting its command channel, preserving executive autonomy, modeling its mechanism, and generating tailored counters. If still finer decomposition is useful, Recursive Sublobar Specialization continues into subsubBrain-lobes and deeper levels.

Generation Example - New Mental Function Invention: The demand need not correspond to a previously named ability. An Arthitean may encounter a problem for which the useful response is a genuinely new mind-performable function, such as simultaneously reasoning under several incompatible causal systems while preventing cross-contamination between them. If no existing lobe or crossfunction adequately supplies that function, Brain-lobe Generation can create architecture specifically for it.

Generation Optimization Implication: Brain-lobe Generation Optimization prevents this open-endedness from requiring a wasteful new primary lobe for every microscopic variation. A newly generated lobe tends toward an architecture capable of developing future subfunctions internally. For example, a broad Mental-Sovereignty Brain-lobe might later develop subBrain-lobes for mind-control resistance, possession resistance, false-memory discrimination, compulsion resistance, and identity preservation instead of generating five unrelated primary lobes when one extensible architecture is more suitable.

Brain-lobe Generation Optimization is the tendency for a newly generated Brain-lobe to possess a functional architecture most likely to accommodate future subfunctions through internal sublobar development rather than requiring further primary Brain-lobes.

Brain-lobe Neural Amount is the amount of neurons functionally available to a Brain-lobe.

Brain-lobe Functional Demandingness is the extent of genuine functional demand imposed upon a Brain-lobe's function, regardless of whether that function is cognitive, metacognitive, defensive, resistant, regulatory, adversarial, self-modifying, power-related, perceptual, identity-related, or any other mind-performable function.

Brain-lobe Exertion is the extent of physiological and neural activity performed by a Brain-lobe and its subdivisions while performing its function.

Brain-lobe Efficiency is the functional effectiveness produced by a Brain-lobe per extent of Brain-lobe Exertion.

Brain-lobe Processing Power is the full extent of simultaneous neural processing a Brain-lobe can perform toward its function.

Brain-lobe Processing Speed is the speed at which a Brain-lobe performs the neural processing required for its function.

Brain-lobe Intraconnectivity is the extent and quality of functionally useful neural connectivity among neurons, circuits, subBrain-lobes, and recursively deeper subdivisions within a Brain-lobe.

InterBrain-lobe Connectivity is the extent and quality of functionally useful neural communication between different Brain-lobes and their subdivisions.

InterBrain-lobe Selectivity is the precision with which interBrain-lobe communication is activated, inhibited, weighted, amplified, or excluded according to functional relevance.

Crossfunctionality is the production of a mental function through the combined functions of two or more Brain-lobes.

Crossfunctionality Scope Clarification: Crossfunctionality is equally unrestricted by ordinary cognitive categories. A useful mental function can emerge from the coordinated operation of lobes whose primary functions are very different. Therefore, Arthitean brains can obtain extraordinary functions either by generating a dedicated lobe, by combining existing lobes into a crossfunction, or by using both approaches together.

Crossfunction Example - Mental Sovereignty: A Mind-Control-Resistance lobe, Identity-Preservation lobe, Neural-Inhibition lobe, and Metaneural-Control lobe could combine into a Mental-Sovereignty crossfunction that detects hostile mental influence, verifies the Arthitean's own executive state, suppresses imposed commands, and restores normal control. If this crossfunction later proves inadequate as a unified function, Brain-lobe Generation can still produce a new primary lobe specialized around the unmet portion.

Crossfunction Example - Adversarial Countercognition: A deception-resistance lobe, opponent-modeling lobe, counterprediction lobe, nullpattern-recognition lobe, and antaintelligence-related lobe could crossfunction to resist being outsmarted while simultaneously modeling how an opponent is trying to manipulate the Arthitean's model of the situation.

Crossfunction Example - Metaeffect Defense: An effect-discrimination lobe, hax-control lobe, superhax-interaction lobe, identity-preservation lobe, and metaneural-control lobe could combine to recognize that an enemy effect is altering the operation of another effect, determine which internal functions are threatened, route protected processing around the interference, and coordinate a mental counterresponse.

Crossfunctional Integration is the capacity of differently functioning Brain-lobes to combine their functions into coherent crossfunctions.

Crossfunctional Efficiency is the effectiveness of crossfunctional processing per extent of neural resources and exertion used to coordinate the contributing Brain-lobes.

Neural Routing is the capacity of the Arthitean brain to dynamically direct, prioritize, suppress, amplify, and reorganize information flow among neurons, subBrain-lobes, Brain-lobes, and higher-order neural systems.

Neural Inhibition is the capacity of Arthitean neural systems to suppress or constrain irrelevant, erroneous, counterproductive, interfering, or otherwise inappropriate neural activity.

Brain-lobe Organization Quality is how effectively the neurons, connections, circuits, subBrain-lobes, and other internal structures of a Brain-lobe are arranged and coordinated for its function.

Brain-lobe Functional Independence is the capacity of a Brain-lobe to perform its function without unnecessary dependence upon functionally unrelated Brain-lobes.

Metaneural Control is neural regulation that monitors, evaluates, selects, modifies, inhibits, amplifies, or redirects the operation of other neural systems.

Brain-lobe Plasticity is the capacity of a Brain-lobe to alter its neuron allocation, organization, connectivity, subdivisions, routing, or other function-relevant architecture.

Function-Specific Neuroplasticity is preferential development of neural properties causally relevant to improving a function in response to demand or difficulty placed upon that function.

Error-Targeted Neuroplasticity is preferential development or reorganization of neural properties causally responsible for a mental-functional failure, bottleneck, inefficiency, weakness, vulnerability, resistance failure, control failure, or other failure in any Brain-lobe function or crossfunction.

Brain-lobe Growth Efficiency is useful function-relevant neural development per extent of Brain-lobe growth.
