17. Strict Logical Deductions

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 280 of 317

IDDeduction
S1H_n cannot be reduced to more of H1 on one scalar because recursive signed coordinates retain typed identity.
S2The coordinate-wise equality criterion proves H_n != H_m and I_n != I_m whenever n != m.
S3-I_k has identity as negative occupancy of the specific basis-symbol tau_I,k; -I_k != -I_j for k != j.
S4If H_n contains H1, primitive Function, Adeptness, Usefulness, and Reliability remain present at 100%; higher order does not raise those primitive percentages above 100%.
S5Function, Adeptness, Usefulness, and Reliability are non-identical axes.
S6Unfunction is not equivalent to non-firing, and Asuncion is not equivalent to Unfunction.
S7A1(X) activation implies X is not firing, but raw A1 does not imply task failure unless d_X=1.
S8MRD=k implies x0...x_(k-1)=0 and x_k=1.
S9Under the failure-gated recurrence, MRD=k>=2 implies r1...r_(k-1)=0 and r_k=1.
S10Therefore high MRD diagnoses recursive Antilobe-monitoring failure/recovery, not increasing positive-function mismatch depth.
S11Positive functional/deployment mismatch requires separate variables such as G_H and CoverageGap.
S12The whole Higher-Lobe family can be structurally available while task-specific D, R, X, and coverage remain variable.
S13Hyperfunction order h, monitoring-recovery depth a, sublobar depth s, and factor vector B are distinct coordinates.
S14The 22-factor vector is not identical to Hyperfunction order; factors alter deployment/coverage/performance envelope without redefining H_n.
S15Crossfunction possibility-space can grow combinatorially without every combination being simultaneously active.
S16PHK includes all twenty-two factors at every PHK-number under the v140 inclusion rule.
S17Universal PHK factor inclusion does not entail equal local or numerical factor growth rates.
S18Brain-lobe Generation expands function-kinds; Higher-Lobe recursion expands orders within an already-used function-kind.
S19Under the canonical zig-zag rule, a newly added negative Infrafunction coordinate has both a purificatory role and a generative role; reducing its meaning to resistance/purification alone is incomplete.
S20Primitive Adeptness remains at 100% while higher Adeptness-like dimensions can be added as distinct typed properties. Therefore higher-order skill dimensionality does not require primitive Adeptness >100%.
S21The new coordinate Z_n=-I_n is not itself identical to the higher-order Adeptness-like property it helps generate. Z_n is an input/state coordinate; A_star,n+1 is an emergent output dimension produced through interaction closure.
S22DirectWidth/CoreWidth counts explicit recursive H/I coordinates only. It is not the total expanded dimensionality after emergent closure.
S23Because A_star,n+1 is canonically type-distinct from primitive A and earlier A_star dimensions while remaining Adeptness-like, the Adeptness-directed property family can strictly expand with recursive Hyperfunction order.
