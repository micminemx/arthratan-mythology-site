Axiomatic Perfect hetu-attributability Ladder The Axiomatic Perfect hetu-attributability Ladder

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 68 of 317

= the recursive ladder measuring how deeply a partial accomplishment or accomplishment = perfectly hetu-wise attributable-to bloodline blessings through axioms and higher-order metaaxioms.

Level 1: the partial accomplishment or accomplishment is perfectly hetu-wise attributable-to.

bloodline blessings axiomatically. Level 2: the partial accomplishment or accomplishment is perfectly hetu-wise attributable-to bloodline blessings axiomatically and metaaxiomatically. Level 3: the partial accomplishment or accomplishment = perfectly hetu-wise attributable-to bloodline blessings axiomatically, metaaxiomatically, and metaaxiomatic^2. Level 4: the partial accomplishment or accomplishment = perfectly hetu-wise attributable-to bloodline blessings axiomatically, metaaxiomatically, metaaxiomatic^2, and metaaxiomatic^3. In general, Level n means the partial accomplishment or accomplishment = perfectly hetu-wise attributable-to bloodline blessings at every level from the axiomatic level up to metaaxiomatic^(n-1). Every deeper level necessarily includes all shallower levels. Axiomatic.
