The central mechanism: intelligence parasynthesis

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 77 of 317

A suitable Ultraenglish term is parasynthesis:

Intelligence parasynthesis is the development and integration of multiple intelligences caused by the need to model, counter, and surpass cognitive agents who can employ those intelligences adversarially.

Antaintelligence does not necessarily create every intelligence from nothing. Instead, it:

stimulates their development;

imports their useful functions;

coordinates them;

improves transfer between them;

constructs higher-order combinations.

For example:

\[ \text{Social intelligence} + \text{predictive intelligence} + \text{strategic intelligence} \rightarrow \text{social-strategic opponent modelling} \]

Antaintelligence creates pressure not merely for more intelligence, but for greater interintelligence interoperability.
