Performance Factor One

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 34 of 317

Performance Factor One is the Lydrextent of Transperformance done per extent of physiological exertion.

Performance Factor One applies exhaustively to every bodily system, organ, tissue, cell, subcellular structure, and physiological process involved in producing performance.

Accordingly, physiological exertion occurring at any level of the Arthitean's physiology can produce Transperformance.

The degree of contraction of a muscle fibre is one example of physiological exertion.
