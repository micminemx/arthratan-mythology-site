No capacity level is a final ceiling: however high a capacity becomes, legitimate superior capacity levels can still exist. A contest is therefore resolved through relative superiority in the capability relevant to the interaction rather than through an assumed final capacity ceiling. Contest superiority differential is the relative difference between opposing parties in the performance metric or combination of performance metrics relevant to an interaction.

A favourable contest superiority differential means one party is increasingly able to outperform, overpower, outspeed, outmaneuver, outlast, or otherwise overwhelm the opposition in the relevant respect.

A zero contest superiority differential means neither party possesses a relative advantage in the performance metric or combination of performance metrics being compared.

Transcendent performance is the Lydrextent of performance. The higher the Lydrextent of Transcendent performance, the higher the performance-level the Arthitean can express and the greater their capacity to outperform or otherwise overwhelm opposition in performance-dependent interactions.
