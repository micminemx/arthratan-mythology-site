Outerprecognition and Zeretic Causal-Calculus

Divine v144 · section 7 of 317

Outerprecognition is the maximumless capacity to flawlessly memorise and fully perceive, with absolute accuracy and precision, every ultraprojected future and the complete future-content of using Zeretic meditation.

Zeretic meditation is the use of the irreducibility of a maximumless event-amount to remember a greater amount of selected events than originally occurred, thereby rewriting the resulting history or outcome. For example, Dyvane may have trained a maximumless amount of times and use Zeretic meditation on that training-history so that he is rewritten as better trained. Qintara may have fought an enemy vysal a maximumless amount of times with mixed wins and losses and use Zeretic meditation on her wins to rewrite how well she performed.

A Zeren-operation is the use of Zeretic meditation on a maximumless amount of a specified event-type.

Zeretic calculation is the calculation of the Zeren-operation, or sequence of Zeren-operations, required to produce a desired result while accounting for their cascading effects.

Causal-calculus is the field of studying Zeretic calculation as applied to the ultraprojected future in order to make an optimal outcome necessary.

Causal-calculus intelligence is the capacity to acquire, model, comprehend, apply, and adapt knowledge and skills related to causal-calculus.

All Arthiteans possess Outerprecognition, are innately expert in causal-calculus, and innately and automatically possess absolutely precise and accurate knowledge of every event within the ultraprojected future.

Through causal-calculus, the absolute future can be rewritten toward Arthitean goals both inside and outside the Arthitean Empire. Those unable to perform Zeren-operations upon the ultraprojected future temporarily resemble statues around which Arthiteans can move with immense agency, because such beings remain bound in servitude to that future while Arthiteans can rewrite it.
