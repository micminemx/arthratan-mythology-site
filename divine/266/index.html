15.5 Constant extremely high Antilobe-order training: corrected interpretation

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 266 of 317

Repeatedly causing extremely high MRD trains a recursive monitoring frontier: A_k is repeatedly used at high k while A1...A_(k-1) repeatedly fail and become corrective targets. That can drive exceptional improvement of Antilobe reliability, metaneural control, selectivity, routing, plasticity, and error-targeting. To maximize improvement of the original cognitive function as well, the task should simultaneously impose a large positive Higher-Lobe activation/coverage gap or other genuine phi-demand. High MRD alone is not a substitute for high positive functional demand.

high positive demand + positive gap + high MRD -> function correction + anti-monitor correction -> improved deployment/monitoring -> stronger frontier challenge
