Transmodal Fitness and Performance Factors

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 17 of 317

Unlimited fitness is fitness without an upper limit within the possibility-space of fitness.

Transmodal-fitness is superior to unlimited fitness because being fitter than unlimited fitness is impossible within that possibility-space, whereas Transmodal-fitness successfully performs at performance-levels whose possibility is denied. It therefore does not quantitatively extend unlimited fitness; it categorically transcends the possibility-framework within which unlimited fitness is unlimited.

Performance factor one is the Lydrextent of Transmodal-performance extent gained per physiological exertion. It applies to every bodily system, organ, and cell and to every kind of performance, including but not limited to intelligence, stamina, endurance, durability, strength, and willpower. A muscle fibre’s degree of contraction is one example of physiological exertion.

Performance factor two is the Lydrextent of Transmodal-exertion extent gained per extent of effort exerted by the Arthitean.

Transmodal-fitness is the state of being able to mentally, physically, spiritually, conceptually perform at any impossible level of performance no matter how impossible or antipossible it is in a way that is more healthy for the user the higher the level of performance.

A test for distinguishing Transmodal-fitness from merely unlimited fitness is that, once the possessor exerts themselves, they are not destiny-bound to any future prediction regardless of that prediction’s quality.

Transmodal-strength is superior to literally unlimited strength.

Rhayhara possesses Transmodal physical prowess, Transmodal mental prowess, and Transmodal spiritual prowess.
