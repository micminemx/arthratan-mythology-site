1.1.2 Extended Brain-lobe function catalogue - Divine v140 established scope and examples

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 98 of 317

The following list deliberately overstates the breadth already licensed or explicitly exemplified by Divine v140. Items are phrased as function-kinds rather than as claims that every Arthitean must always recruit a dedicated primary lobe for each item. A function may instead be implemented by a sublobe, crossfunction, temporary network, or generated lobe depending on architectural usefulness.

Familiar cognition and metacognition

- Reasoning: performing inference, comparison, abstraction, and problem-solving operations.

- Memory: encoding, retaining, retrieving, organizing, or reconciling stored information.

- Attention: selecting, sustaining, dividing, or reallocating mental focus.

- Language: comprehending, producing, structuring, translating, or controlling linguistic information.

- Planning: constructing and updating action sequences, goals, prerequisites, and contingencies.

- Emotion processing or regulation: representing, interpreting, modulating, or strategically using emotional states.

- Perception: interpreting sensory or analogous mental inputs and converting them into usable representations.

- Metacognitive monitoring: thinking about, supervising, evaluating, or correcting other thinking processes.

Mental sovereignty, resistance, and defensive functions

- Mind-control resistance: resisting foreign executive commands and preserving autonomous choice.

- Intrusion detection: detecting foreign mental commands, hostile cognitive channels, or unauthorized executive influence.

- Command isolation: separating hostile commands from legitimate internally generated intentions.

- Executive self-authentication: verifying that a command, intention, or decision genuinely belongs to the Arthitean self.

- Hostile-signal inhibition: suppressing hostile control signals before they can propagate through the wider neural system.

- Adaptive counter-resistance: learning from previously successful control methods and generating increasingly tailored resistance.

- Identity preservation: preserving who is making decisions despite attempts to rewrite or replace identity.

- Personality-rewriting resistance: resisting forced alteration of personality architecture.

- False-memory discrimination: distinguishing authentic memories from inserted, altered, fabricated, or adversarially edited memories.

- Memory-based identity-alteration resistance: preventing memory manipulation from silently changing self-identity or executive authority.

- Possession resistance: preventing an external entity from acquiring executive control over the mind.

- Imposed-persona rejection: detecting and rejecting externally imposed personas or identity overlays.

- Consciousness-fragmentation containment: preserving or restoring coherent agency when consciousness is split, fragmented, or partitioned.

- Compulsion resistance: resisting forced urges, commands, or decision-biasing effects.

- Anti-manipulation processing: detecting, resisting, or neutralizing attempts to manipulate cognition, emotion, decision-making, or identity.

- Anti-hax neural defence: mentally resisting, filtering, or countering hax that operate through or upon the mind/neural system.

Cognitohazard, memetic, and informational-safety functions

- Cognitohazard neutralization: processing mentally dangerous information without allowing its hazardous effect to operate normally.

- Memetic-contamination resistance: preventing hostile or self-propagating informational patterns from spreading through cognition.

- Dangerous-representation quarantine: isolating hazardous mental representations from vulnerable neural systems.

- Harmless re-encoding: transforming dangerous information into a safe internal representation while preserving useful content.

- Propagation inhibition: preventing a hazardous representation from automatically reproducing across other Brain-lobes or crossfunctions.

- Safe hazardous-information comprehension: allowing useful analysis of dangerous information without surrendering mental integrity.

Telepathic and thought-reading counterintelligence

- Telepathic-extraction resistance: preventing unauthorized reading or extraction of internal information.

- Thought obscuration: reducing the accessibility or interpretability of selected thoughts to hostile readers.

- Decoy mental-content generation: creating deliberately misleading but controlled thought-content for adversarial observers.

- Telepathic-signal authentication: distinguishing legitimate incoming telepathic information from forged or hostile transmissions.

- Selective mental disclosure: exposing only deliberately chosen information while protecting everything else.

Impossible, alien, conceptual, and non-human cognition

- Paradoxical-structure comprehension: representing and reasoning about paradoxical structures without ordinary cognitive collapse.

- Alien cognition: comprehending modes of thought whose structure has no human cognitive analogue.

- Conceptually impossible-structure comprehension: mentally operating on structures that ordinary conceptual systems would reject as impossible.

- Higher-ontological comprehension: representing or reasoning about structures located at unfamiliar or higher ontological layers.

- Maximumless-structure comprehension: reasoning about structures whose relevant amounts or organizational depth have no terminal maximum.

- Alien-mode emulation: temporarily emulating an alien mode of thought while preventing that temporary architecture from destabilizing the ordinary self.

- Incompatible-causal-system reasoning: simultaneously reasoning under several incompatible causal systems while preventing cross-contamination between them.

Power, hax, and metaeffect control

- Power control: precisely controlling a supernatural or otherwise nonordinary capability through neural operations.

- Multipower coordination: coordinating many powers simultaneously as a unified but selectively controllable system.

- Hostile-effect discrimination: distinguishing hostile effects from harmless, allied, self-generated, or desired effects.

- Hax-interaction management: mentally managing how hax interact, conflict, combine, resist, or bypass one another.

- Metaeffect control: controlling metaeffects whose direct operational targets are other effects, their production, operation, properties, persistence, applicability, interaction, or outcome.

Temporal and distributed-self functions

- Time-loop memory preservation: preserving usable memory through repeated or altered temporal cycles.

- Timeline-memory discrimination: distinguishing memories originating from different timelines, temporal states, or rewritten histories.

- Distributed-avatar information integration: integrating information from avatars, copies, or distributed manifestations of the self.

- Concurrent-consciousness coordination: coordinating multiple simultaneous streams of consciousness without losing task integration or identity control.

Maximumless neural coordination

- Neural addressing: locating and directing signals to relevant neural structures within an enormously or maximumlessly large internal architecture.

- Maximumless parallelism management: coordinating maximumlessly many possible or active parallel neural operations without treating them as one undifferentiated workload.

- Maximumless attention allocation: selecting and distributing attention across a maximumless-scale candidate space.

- Maximumless crossfunctionality management: selecting, organizing, and regulating crossfunctions in an open-ended or maximumless network possibility-space.

- Maximumless simultaneous-operation coordination: coordinating maximumlessly many simultaneous neural operations while preserving routing, relevance, and functional separation.
