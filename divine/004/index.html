Maximumless Amount

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 4 of 317

Mul is the maximumless amount of possible successive revolutions around a circle, such that for every specified amount of revolutions, a greater amount remains possible. Mul is an amount because “the amount of possible times one can revolve around a circle” is coherent.

Hyperirresistible is the state of being intrinsically irresistible and irresistibly intrinsic. As hyperirresistibleness increases, irresistibleness, the intrinsicness of that irresistibleness, and the irresistibleness of that intrinsicness all increase.

Hax are confrontation-relevant abilities whose effects circumvent, override, invalidate, exploit, bypass, or otherwise render one or more ordinary physical, mental, spiritual, or analogous statistics insufficiently decisive.

All hax produce effects.

Metaeffect: an effect whose direct operational target is another effect, its production, operation, properties, persistence, applicability, interaction, or outcome

Superhax

Superhax are hax whose primary effect(s) are metaeffects

Therefore:

- All superhax are hax.

- All hax produce effects.

- All superhax produce metaeffects.

- Not all hax produce metaeffects.

- Not every metaeffect-producing ability qualifies as superhax, because it must also satisfy the condition of beiny a hax.

Power skewity is the directional and magnitudinal imbalance of a character’s confrontation-capability across statistics, hax, and superhax.

Hax quality considers nature of possessed hax, hax depth of hax, hax breadth and the strength of their hax

Superhax quality considers nature of possessed Superhax, Superhax depth of Superhax, Superhax breadth and the strength of their Superhax

Superhax wouldn't be useless against a purely physical opponent because physical attack nullification and physical attack reflection are superhax etc etc there are so many more superhax that can work

Positive power skewity: statistics < hax < superhax.(where one possesses superior collective superhax quality than their collective hax quality and their collective stats quality and they posses superior collective hax quality than their collective stats quality )

Negative power skewity: statistics > hax > superhax.(where one possesses superior collective stats quality than their collective hax quality and their collective superhax quality and they posses superior collective hax quality than their collective superhax quality)

Nonlinear power skewity: any unequal arrangement satisfying neither positive nor negative power skewity.

Zero power skewity: statistics = hax = superhax.
