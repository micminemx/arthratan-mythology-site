22. AI-Parser Anti-Misunderstanding Guide

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 293 of 317

- Rule 1 - Do not parse Brain-lobe as cognition-only: A Brain-lobe is specialized for a distinct Arthitean mental/neural function, and that function-space explicitly includes defensive, resistant, adversarial, regulatory, identity-preserving, power-controlling, anti-hax, temporal, conceptual, maximumless, and alien functions.

- Rule 2 - Do not substitute external effect for mental function: The eligible lobe-function is the neural operation that performs, controls, regulates, resists, simulates, preserves, or coordinates something. An unrelated external event is not automatically a lobe-function.

- Rule 3 - Do not restrict Higher Hyperfunction to intelligence: Every used eligible function-kind can possess its own Higher-Lobe family. Higher Hyperfunction order is function-general, not cognition-exclusive.

- Rule 4 - Do not restrict Hyperemergent Crossfunctionality to cognitive-cognitive interactions: Hyperemergence can combine higher-order emergent dimensions from any eligible function classes, including cognition x defence, identity x temporal, hax x conceptual, metaneural x maximumless coordination, and far stranger combinations.

- Rule 5 - Keep source-canon examples separate from illustrative extensions: Divine v140 explicitly establishes the broad scope and many examples. This edition adds further boundary-pushing examples to demonstrate consequences of that scope; those extensions should not be misquoted as verbatim Divine v140 examples.
