9.9 Complete monitoring failure

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 184 of 317

If x0=0 and r_k=0 for every anti-layer considered, then no first active Antilobe exists in that analyzed range. This is different from "very high MRD": very high MRD means a late monitor eventually succeeded; complete monitoring failure means none did.
