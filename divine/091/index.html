Important qualification

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 91 of 317

General intelligence would not automatically rise faster merely because someone performs competitive deception repeatedly. The effect depends on whether the person genuinely:

learns;

comprehends;

models;

transfers;

adapts;

corrects themselves;

encounters sufficiently diverse cognitive agents.

Otherwise, they could develop narrow antagocognitive habits without large general-intelligence growth.

Therefore, the strongest formulation is:

Well-developed antaintelligence has a superproportional general-intelligence yield because increasingly advanced antagocognition requires the development, coordination, and recursive optimisation of cognition-general capacities.

This also explains why antaintelligence would be rare. Many people possess outsmarting-skills, but few possess the deeper learning-intelligence that converts every cognitive contest into broad cognitive-system development.
