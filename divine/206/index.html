11.15 Heterohypercrossfunctionality - different-function Hyperemergent Crossfunctionality

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 206 of 317

Heterohypercrossfunctionality is Hyperemergent Crossfunctionality among strongly different function-kinds. The participants need not resemble one another. Their difference may actually enlarge the interaction-space because the network gains access to emergent dimensions drawn from unlike functional families.

phi != psi AND Similar(phi,psi)=0 or low

Expanded(L_phi,h1) x Expanded(L_psi,h2) -> Heterohypercrossfunction

For example, H300 creativity, H500 temporal perception, and H90 deception-resistance could crossfunction. A hyperemergent output might use a creativity-generated dimension to produce candidate possibilities, a temporal-perception-generated dimension to structure them across time, and a deception-resistance-generated dimension to reject adversarially contaminated candidates. The resulting function need not be reducible to creativity, temporal perception, or deception-resistance individually.

Heterofunctionality does not guarantee greater novelty than homofunctionality or parafunctionality. It merely describes functional dissimilarity of the contributors. Whether it produces more valuable, broader, deeper, stranger, or more irreducible hyperemergence is an empirical/canonical question dependent on the actual emergence mapping and neural architecture.
