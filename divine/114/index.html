2.8 Four distinct questions

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 114 of 317

AxisQuestionExample with singing
FunctionIs the neural structure performing singing rather than another/opposed function?It is actually singing.
AdeptnessHow skillfully/proficiently is the singing function executed?Pitch, control, timing, interpretation, technique.
UsefulnessDoes this singing function beneficially contribute to the intended result?It achieves the relevant musical/communicative purpose.
ReliabilityDoes good singing performance persist under destabilizing conditions?It remains high-quality under distraction, interference, novelty, pressure.

Function answers WHAT. Adeptness answers HOW SKILLFULLY. Usefulness answers WHETHER IT HELPS. Reliability answers WHETHER QUALITY HOLDS UNDER THREAT.
