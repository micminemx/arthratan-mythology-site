24. Open Variables Not Yet Fixed by Canon

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 311 of 317

Open questionWhat is already fixedWhat remains unspecified
Are Higher-Lobe orders structurally available or generated only when needed?Resolved in this revision: for every used function, the corresponding Higher-Lobe family is structurally available without terminal order.The precise biological mechanism by which that standing family is physically distributed across maximumless cranial architecture need not be specified for the logic.
Which structurally available H-orders does a particular task recruit?Task demand D, recruitment R, and activation X are distinct sets; only demanded/recruited orders are task-relevant.The exact biological selection algorithm mapping task-state to D/R/X remains open and can be governed by routing/metaneural control.
Does deeper MRD automatically cause more development?MRD>=2 definitively means additional lower Antilobe monitoring layers failed before a later sentinel succeeded.The quantitative law mapping MRD to anti-monitoring correction remains optional; positive-function correction must use separate deficits.
Can Antilobes have their own Hyperfunction ladder as an anti-monitoring function?Antilobes are lobes and can receive factor-based development.Whether a separate H_n ladder for the Antilobe's own function is explicitly canon.
Do Adeptness/Usefulness/Reliability have neutral states?Positive and opposed states exist.No neutral states defined.
Is there a universal factor threshold for H_n?Architecture must be sufficient to realize H_n.No universal per-factor fi(h) map.
How many H orders does a task recruit?A demand profile can be defined.Exact recruitment law.

Leaving these explicit is preferable to silently filling them with assumptions.
