Omnitimeline and Transfuture Ladder

Divine v144 · section 6 of 317

Omnitimeline is the ordered sequence of complete omniversal temporal states generated through successive rewrites of the omniverse’s causal-temporal structure.

A transfuture is an omniversal temporal state situated at a later Omnitimeline index than a specified reference index.

Transfuture-distance is the amount of successive omniversal temporal rewrites separating a transfuture from its specified reference index.

A projected transfuture is a projected later Omnitimeline state or Omnitimeline-region produced by projecting forward through successive possible temporal rewrites from a specified Omnitimeline reference index.

A metaprojected transfuture is the projected transfuture of the recalibrated projected transfuture.

A metaprojected² transfuture is the projected transfuture of the recalibrated metaprojected transfuture.

A metaprojected³ transfuture is the projected transfuture of the recalibrated metaprojected² transfuture.
