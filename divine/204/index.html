11.13 Homohypercrossfunctionality - same-function Hyperemergent Crossfunctionality

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 204 of 317

Homohypercrossfunctionality is Hyperemergent Crossfunctionality among participating lobes or Higher-Lobe orders that share the same function-kind. This is possible because different Hyperfunction orders of the same function retain function-kind identity while possessing nonidentical typed recursive and emergent dimensional structure.

FunctionKind(L_phi,h1)=FunctionKind(L_phi,h2)=phi, while Expanded(H_h1(phi)) != Expanded(H_h2(phi)) when h1 != h2

Expanded(L_phi,h1) x Expanded(L_phi,h2) -> Homohypercrossfunction

The output need not be redundant simply because both contributors are "doing the same function." H5 strategy and H100 strategy are both strategy, but H100 contains a richer family of higher-order dimensions. Their interaction can integrate differently ordered realizations of strategy into a network property no single participating order produces alone. This could include simultaneous strategy at multiple recursive resolutions, mutual correction between order-specific strategy dimensions, order-bridging abstraction, or another strategy-directed property. Exact phenotypes remain function-specific rather than predetermined by the mathematics.

Homohypercrossfunctionality is therefore not "two copies of the same answer." It is potentially an interaction among different dimensional implementations of the same function-kind.
