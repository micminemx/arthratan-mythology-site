1.3 Recursive sublobar specialization

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 103 of 317

A SubBrain-lobe specializes in a subfunction. Recursive Sublobar Specialization allows subsubBrain-lobes and recursively deeper subdivisions to perform progressively more specific subfunctions.
