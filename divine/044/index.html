Throne of Destiny Transcendence = a hypervysal throne that allows an Arthitean who sits upon it to sense the intelligences they need, if they do not already possess them, and to sense the magnitude of each required intelligence. At the throne itself, the Arthitean can sense the intelligences required to figure out how to create, metacreate, and/or encounter an opponent so formidable-to Rhayhara that being able to not lose against that opponent would cause massive Black Event Ontological Blessing Decay and would be extremely transadversial. This does not grant the Arthitean those intelligences.

These levels = measured by how many steps away from the throne the Arthitean = standing.

The throne itself = Level 0.

One physical step away from the throne = Level -1.
