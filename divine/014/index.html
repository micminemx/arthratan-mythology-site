Divine v144 Source Corpus

Comparative Performance Outcomes

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 14 of 317

Overwhelmed is the state in which an entity cannot successfully keep up with, resist, or prevail against the effective capability imposed by an opposing entity, system, state, or thing in the relevant interaction.

Overpowering is defeating or overcoming an opponent in a power- or strength-dependent interaction because one possesses superior effective power or strength for that interaction.

Overpowered is the state of being defeated or overcome through the opponent's superior effective power or strength.

Outspeeding is surpassing an opponent in a speed-dependent interaction through superior effective speed.

Outsped is the state of being surpassed in a speed-dependent interaction by an opponent with superior effective speed.

Outmaneuvering is surpassing an opponent in an agility- or maneuverability-dependent interaction through superior effective agility or maneuverability.

Outmaneuvered is the state of being surpassed in an agility- or maneuverability-dependent interaction by an opponent with superior effective agility or maneuverability.

Outlasting is surpassing an opponent in an endurance- or stamina-dependent interaction by sustaining effective performance for longer or under greater adversity.

Outlasted is the state of being surpassed in an endurance- or stamina-dependent interaction by an opponent who sustains effective performance for longer or under greater adversity.

Outperforming is surpassing an opponent in the relevant performance metric or combination of performance metrics.

Outperformed is the state of being surpassed in the relevant performance metric or combination of performance metrics.

Contest superiority differential is the relative difference between opposing parties in the performance metric or combination of performance metrics relevant to an interaction. A larger favourable differential means that one party more strongly outperforms, overpowers, outspeeds, outmaneuvers, outlasts, or otherwise overwhelms the other in the relevant respect.

Overwhelm gradient is the Lydrextent of the degree to which one party overwhelms another in a specified interaction.
