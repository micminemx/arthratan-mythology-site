11.30.5 Recursive crossfunction composition

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 226 of 317

HC_1 = Cross(L_prediction,50, L_simulation,80)

HC_2 = Cross(HC_1, L_strategy,100)

HC_3 = Cross(HC_2, A_2(L_deception-detection,40))

HC_1 has c=1 if it is Hyperemergent. HC_2 has c=2 when it materially depends on HC_1's hyperemergent output. HC_3 has c=3 if it materially depends on HC_2 and additionally integrates an Antilobe signal. h-values of the contributing lobes remain separate from c.
