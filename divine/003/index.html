Capacity is a spendable reserve to do something

Cognitive efficiency is cognitive-task-effectiveness/Cognitive-goal-achievement-effectiveness

per extent of usage of Cognitive capacities

Cognitive capacity the capacity of what someone is cognitively capable of doing

Cognitive Deployability is the capacity of how Cognitive efficiently one can use their own Cognitive capacities to complete goals and accomplish tasks

Cognitive proficiency is Cognitive Deployability per Cognitive capacity (capacity divided by capacity not ability divided by capacity because ability can be an instance in capacity)

Cognitive agility is the capacity to change how or what one thinks, quickly and easily

Cognitive power is the full extent of processing power one can possess or exhibit

Cognitive speed is the full extent of processing speed one can exhibit

Cognitive tasks failure can be divided up into two main error types. Cognitive Deployment error and Cognitive capacity error.(give examples of this errors)

metacognitive efficiency is metacognitive-task-effectiveness/metacognitive-goal-achievement-effectiveness

per extent of usage of metacognitive capacities

metacognitive capacity the capacity of what someone is metacognitively capable of doing

metacognitive Deployability is the capacity of how metacognitive efficiently one can use their own metacognitive capacities to complete goals and accomplish tasks

metacognitive proficiency is metacognitive Deployability per metacognitive capacity (capacity divided by capacity not ability divided by capacity because ability can be an instance in capacity)

metacognitive power is the full extent of metathinking power one can possess or exhibit

metacognitive speed is the full extent of metathinking speed one can exhibit

Metacognitive agility is the capacity to change how or what one metathinks, quickly and easily

This is true for every arthitean For every cognitive capacity that has a corresponding metacognitive capacity. The extent of the given cognitive capacity is equal to the Lydrextent of metacognitive capacity, e. g. cognitive agility and metacognitive agility, cognitive speed and metacognitive speed
