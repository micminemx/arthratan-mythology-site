19. Plausible Emergent Properties and Predictive Consequences

Divine v144 Corpus · Section 282 of 317

Predicted propertyExplanation
Recursive Adeptness-like dimensionalityHigher H orders do not merely resist Unadeptness. Each zig-zag adds a new negative Infrafunction coordinate whose interaction with the inherited Hyperfunctional state generates a type-distinct Adeptness-like dimension. Primitive Adeptness remains 100%, while the broader Adeptness-directed family gains higher-order members.
Omission-aware cognitionPositive functions can use Antilobe signals as inputs, making 'what failed to occur' available to reasoning.
Failure-of-failure awarenessA2+ are explicit neural states showing that lower absence-monitoring layers themselves failed/unavailable before a later sentinel succeeded. This is recursively higher self-monitoring failure information.
Order-specific metaneural schedulingMetaneural Control can choose which H orders are relevant to a task and which anti-signals deserve response.
Adaptive sparsificationSelectivity, routing, inhibition, and Factor 22 can make a huge potential network behave as a sparse, task-specific network.
Architectural frontier detectionA frontier is identified from the joint profile: high demanded H-orders, nonempty activation/coverage gaps, crossfunctional deficits, and - if present - high MRD showing monitoring-chain failure. MRD alone is not the positive frontier metric.
Factor-response profilingTraining can measure which of the 22 factors changed after a specific h/a challenge.
Crossfunction failure localizationIf individual lobes succeed but a crossfunction fails, the target of improvement shifts from local lobe strength to Factors 9-13, 16, 19, and 22.
Anti-complexity scalingFactors 21 and 22 allow larger local/network architecture to become more effective per complexity used.
Improvement of improvementPlasticity, growth efficiency, NFE scaling, factor inclusion in PHK, and targeted diagnostics improve not just function but the machinery of later development.
Purification-generated noveltyRemoving/negatively occupying a richer Infrafunctional structure can change the interaction regime of the positive state. Thus purification is not only protective: it can enable new positive dimensional properties that were unavailable before the relevant negative coordinate existed.
Higher-order Function/Usefulness/Reliability analoguesBy the same interaction logic, the new negative dimensions can plausibly generate Function-like, Usefulness-like, Reliability-like, and mixed higher-order properties. Their exact presence and phenotype are predicted unless/until separately canonized; the Adeptness-like lineage is explicitly established in this revision.
Mixed higher-order functional propertiesSome emergent dimensions may resemble several primitive axes at once rather than belonging neatly to one family. For example, an emergent property can be simultaneously skill-like, reliability-like, and usefulness-like while remaining irreducible to all three primitive axes.
