How a Myth relates to a transmission

Editorial canon rule: narrative elaboration may determine how an established canonical event is told, but it may not change what canonical event occurred.

Zubaida transmissions remain the primary preserved evidence. Myths are the canonical literary realization of that evidence: structured for reading, scene continuity, feat citation, character pages, search and AI retrieval.

Rhayhara's Ten Destiny-Defying Trials

In the infinitely dilated Sehexi room, the Null-Axiom strips away, one by one, the conditions that make endurance possible. Rhayhara answers each completed trial with survival, Pressurised-Enlightenment and a pararetroactive Bloodline Blessing—until the tenth trial confronts her with a Transmaximumless superior mirror of herself.
