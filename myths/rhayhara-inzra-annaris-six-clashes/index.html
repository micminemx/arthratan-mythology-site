Rhayhara, Inzra and Annaris: Six Metaprojected Clashes

Six confrontations change the relation under attack: raw infinite-capacity pressure becomes resource bankruptcy; Blessing attribution becomes a legal-defense problem; two Disasters deadlock each other; recursive Superhax is rejected at admissibility; isolation is erased from parahistory; and a final combined Meta-destiny offensive is collapsed into a counterable state.

I · Rhayhara vs Inzra

Bankrupt the infinite attack

In Ke'enmon Subdimension 4, Inzra opens with a hypercapacity attack the source describes as unlimited in raw destructive extent and carrying the mathematical weight of infinite transcapacity. Rhayhara explicitly refuses an endless raw-force contest. Instead she changes the economy governing the attack.

By rewriting Greater-Orders permission parameters, she imposes a negative overcostedness differential: maintaining the attack and Inzra's own existence inside the contest becomes unsustainably expensive. The attack collapses through conceptual debt rather than a stated greater-infinity blast.

The victory is explicit but local: the source calls it absolute in that specific Omnitimeline index, then immediately says Inzra's nozrifuture integration recalibrates from the defeat and generates a harder future hyperchallenge.

II · Annaris vs Rhayhara

Defend attribution itself

Annaris avoids physical statistics and targets the metaaxioms that make Rhayhara's Bloodline Blessings attributable to her. Black Event Ontological Blessing Decay would make previous and future feats unaxiomatically attributable, stripping agency without striking her body.

Rhayhara uses the Throne of Destiny Transcendence diagnostically rather than as a power source, calculates the required permission depth and rewrites Z0 through Z3. She retroactively acquires Mythic Blessings structurally immune to the decay and renders Annaris's Level-4 Superhax legally void under the new parameters.

III · Inzra vs Annaris

The Zun-state

In Talmorai the two Disasters turn their adaptive systems against one another. Annaris builds an Antiunsurpassable loop in which Inzra's attempts to improve increase Annaris's relative advantage. Inzra answers with maximumless Meta-destiny² interference and contradictory destinies.

The source does not award either a victory. For every Zefir of advantage Annaris gains, Inzra's nozrifuture integration and Meta-destiny interference match it. Their clash becomes a Zun-state stalemate—equality of outcome in this interaction, not proof of equality in every stat.

IV · Rhayhara vs adapted Inzra

Reject the recursive Superhax

Inzra returns having learned from the first defeat. He no longer attacks raw capacity; a recursive Superhax targets the production and persistence of Rhayhara's powers before manifestation.

Rhayhara changes target-level. Rosha and Venshen attack the legality of the metaeffect chain, raising the admissibility conflict to Z4. The source says reality rejects Inzra's Superhax rather than permit a contradiction at that level, shattering the chain. The victory is therefore an admissibility/metaeffect result, not evidence that every recursive effect in every external ontology automatically fails.

V · Rhayhara vs Annaris

Erase the isolation barrier from parahistory

Annaris erects an absolute conceptual isolation barrier intended to sever Rhayhara's metaphysical, emotional and conceptual links to the empire. Rhayhara invokes Akesi Synergy metaconceptually, concentrating the mutual loyalty of the Top-50 clan leaders into a Nakosh flame.

Through Modus-Tollens logic, vonin and a Nay'ruon calculation, the source says the flame does not merely break the barrier: the confrontation's parahistory is rewritten so the barrier could never have existed. The clans retain the stress-memory of the erased isolation, which the narrative then treats as a zirunstressableness catalyst.

VI · Rhayhara vs Inzra + Annaris

Collapse the combined Meta-destiny attack

In Ke'enmon Subdimension 5, Inzra and Annaris temporarily combine forces. Their synchronized Recursive Meta-destiny attack seeks to make Rhayhara's survival or victory mathematically and conceptually impossible across timelines.

Rhayhara moves to the NDMladder and, from that source-described relation, collapses the recursive Meta-destiny⁴ attack into a finite, predictable and counterable state. She then uses an idempotent Ultradivinity strike to erase the specific nozrifuture indices in which the Inzra–Annaris alliance could logically threaten her again.

This is a direct combined-opponent event. It does not imply that every future individual state of Inzra or Annaris is globally below every Rhayhara statistic, nor does native "infinite" language supply an external cardinal without a bridge.

CROSSSCALE-ONLY / NONCANON ANALYTICAL CONSTRUCT

What the six clashes prove

The six-session chain is strongest as a relation-diversity feat: resource sustainability, attribution legality, adversarial stalemate, metaeffect admissibility, parahistorical isolation and Meta-destiny outcome control are distinct predicates.

Compact result: Rhayhara has direct event-edges over Inzra, Annaris and their combined attack architecture in this transmission; Inzra and Annaris have a specific Zun-state stalemate with each other. These edges must remain encounter- and statdomain-indexed.
