The Protocol imposed an infinite divine-debt, calculating that for Inzra to simply maintain their structural integrity against universal erasure, they would be forced into a permanently overcosted state. The Protocol demanded a hypercapacity spendage that was designed to completely drain any finite reserve.

"You seek to crush a *mul* capacity," Inzra’s voice vibrated through the collapsing layers of Omnihistory and Omnifuture. "You are applying pressure to an absolute, irreducible amount."

As the Protocol attempted to disproportionately spend Inzra’s invulnerability to bypass it, Inzra invoked their Divine Combat.
