Lay’s trap established a crushing overcost gradient; a massive positive divine-debt differential was forced upon Annaris, meant to ensure that she was infinitely overcosted by the opposition.

Yet, Annaris did not flinch. She possessed the Innate hypercapacity potential present in all Arthiteans, which included an absolute Subconscious Capacity Blocks system encompassing mental, physical, and ultraconceptual performance.
