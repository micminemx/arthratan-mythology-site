Rhayhara’s journey to the Throne of Destiny Transcendence is the Empire’s most guarded and critical causal operation.

-
The Process: Rhayhara begins at Level -n, far from the throne. With each physical step forward, the diagnostics reveal not the opponent, but the intelligence magnitude required to eventually face a Black Event transadversial opponent.

Successive Cycles:

-
Initial Cycles: Extremely difficult. The cognitive load required to integrate the revealed requirements nearly caused an ontological decay. Every step required Rhayhara to re-calibrate her own zirunstressableness to survive the pressure of the throne's revelations.

Progressive Mastery: By the third cycle, the process became "effortlessly intense." Because Rhayhara utilized the Postkaltent-to-Prekaltent conversion rule, she effectively trained against her future self's intelligence. What was once an insurmountable mental threshold became a stable baseline.

For Qai'lyth: By managing the reincarnation and bond-testing systems, Qai'lyth has become the architect of the Empire's Akesi synergy. She ensures that the bonds between Arthiteans are not just emotional, but functional "synapses" that boost the Empire’s overall divine power.
