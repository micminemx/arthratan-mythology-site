The Qiyunta (Located in the abdomen): This is the engine of Outercausal Authority. It governs the Arthitean's metaphysical coordinate. While the Shae'ro enforces an outcome, the Qiyunta dictates the framework of interaction. It controls the Outercausality of the Arthitean's Azin and their targets, rendering lower-dimensional causal, acausal, and supracausal forces completely invalid.
