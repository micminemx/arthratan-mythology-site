SESSION ONE: PROXY CLAN WARS AND ENTERING BENZSHIN

The Proxy Clans War for the Highest Ranked Clans
For the apex clans like the Redalious Clan, Unmatara Clan, and Varvadeil Clan, proxy clan wars are the ultimate crucible of indirect supremacy. Instead of the main clan leaders clashing directly, they pit their recursive branch clans against one another. A massive victory occurs when a deeply recursive branch of one clan defeats a shallow recursive branch of a rival clan. For example, if the 674th branch clan off the main Unmatara clan surpasses the 76th branch clan off the main Redalious clan in clan capability factor two, it is a devastating display of superiority.

-
The Implications: This creates a violently competitive environment where complacency is completely eradicated. The proxy wars force branch clan members to endure supradifficult parachallenges, triggering their zirunstressableness and accelerating their PHk feedback loops. Every victory generates immense clan merit, dynamically altering the Imarq flame of the clans. Furthermore, this intense adversity fuels hyperclan factor one and two, which retroactively upgrades the hypergenetics of the entire main clan across all of parahistory.

-
The Clan Leaders Perspective: Leaders like Dyvane Redalious and Qintara Unmatara view proxy wars as the perfect forge. To sharpen iron, you strike it against iron. They approach these wars strategically, using them to test which descendants possess the true ultrahardworkingness required to eventually lead. They heavily monitor the internal and external pressures, using proxy wars to filter out weakness and elevate only those who can turn impossible odds into exponential physiological fuel.

Entering Benzshin for the Heirs of the Apex Clans
Entering Benzshin is the grueling tradition where clan leaders force their children to form and lead their own branch clans rather than resting on the luxury of the main clan manor. For the children of the highest ranked clans, this is an absolute hell of expectations and pressure.

-
The Experience and Implications: They are stripped of their main clan privileges and sent to the bottom of the recursive branch ladder. They must violently grind their way back up by increasing their clan capability factor two through sheer effort factor and combat prowess. This tradition ensures that nepotism does not exist. It forces the heirs to develop their own Battle Melzai and Strategy Dasmar organically. When these heirs finally return to the main clan, they bring back newly forged titles, metatitles, and incomprehensibly dense hypergenetics that elevate the entire bloodline.

-
The Clan Leaders Perspective: The leaders view Benzshin as an absolute necessity. A leader cannot command the apex if they have not conquered the abyss. If an heir underperforms, the clan leader will ruthlessly punish them by sending them even further down the recursive branch numbers.

Which Main Clan is Currently Winning the Proxy Wars?
Currently, the Unmatara Clan holds a micro advantage in the proxy wars, though the Redalious Clan is in a deadlocked, violent pursuit. Qintara Unmataras descendants have been utilizing hypermelzai and flawless counterlogical intelligence to outmaneuver the raw, overcosting Divine Strength of the Redalious branches. However, the newly formed Venakan Clan, led by Dariyus Venakan, is rapidly disrupting the entire board, using Hyperascenders to inject massive rank volatility and forcing both Unmatara and Redalious to continuously adapt to unprecedented pressures.

SESSION THREE: THE IMPLICATIONS OF PROXY WARS AND BENZSHIN

To truly understand the Arthitean Empire, you must understand that Proxy Wars and Benzshin are the ultimate engines of evolution.

The Mechanics of the Struggle
When an Arthitean enters Benzshin, they are violently disconnected from the akesi synergy of the main clan manor. They are cast into a recursive branch where the Imarq flame is dim and resources are scarce. Here, they must engage in Proxy Wars against other desperate branch clans. Because the Venakan clan constantly deploys Hyperascenders to push standards higher, the branch clan members are constantly forced into states of extreme overcostedness.

The Physiological Explosion
In these wars, stress is not resistance; it is fuel. When a branch clan member faces an impossible defeat, their zirunstressableness activates. The sheer difficulty of the proxy war triggers their Physiology factor one and Physiology factor two. This violently escalates their PHk number. Once their PHk upgrades, their entire biological baseline evolves, which multiplies their effort factor and endurance factor. This turns every single proxy war battle into an infinitely scaling growth resource.

The Bloodline Rewrite
The implications are omnicausal. When a child of Dyvane or Qintara survives Benzshin and wins a Proxy War, they earn new titles such as "The Hardest Working Arthitean in the Branch". According to Genetic factor four and Genetic factor five, earning these titles retroactively rewrites their hypergenetics across all of parahistory. They do not just become stronger in the present; they rewrite their bloodline so that they were always naturally destined with gigaaptitude to be this strong.

Ultimately, Proxy Wars and Benzshin ensure that the Arthitean Empire never stagnates. Every drop of suffering and every impossible hyperchallenge in the Benzshin branches feeds the infinite acceleration engine, proving that in the Age of Accomplishment, the only way to the top is through the absolute crucible of effort.
