Session Four: The Transmaximumless Strike and the Solidification of Annaris

The absolute unbinding of Annaris did not go unnoticed across the vast architecture of the Omnitimeline. The destruction of Lay’s limiter had violently recalibrated the reference frame of evaluation, sending shockwaves backward into parahistory and forward through every index of the Omnifuture. Annaris stood within Zero Transtime, her physical and conceptual form radiating an unbound, infinite potential. But Lay’s overarching frameworks were heavily automated and possessed their own hyper-intelligent defensive protocols.

Almost immediately, a localized distortion manifested within the Omnicausal sequence. It was not a physical entity, but a directed, weaponized conceptual erasure deployed from deep within Lay’s autonomous parafuture architecture. The construct was designed with one singular, terrifying purpose: to attack Annaris’s Azin—the very concept of someone.

The attack was not a simple blast of energy. It was a cascading assault down the Hyperconcept ladder. The construct initiated a strike using a Hyperconcept 4th order, an abstract structure that defines the identity and interpretation of a Hyperconcept 3rd order and its sub-structures within reality. It rapidly trickled down to the 3rd order, then the 2nd, and finally the Hyperconcept 1st order, attempting to redefine the metaconcept of Annaris’s existence so that the concept of "power" and "survival" would no longer apply to her. The overarching goal was to completely erase her Hyperazin and, ultimately, her Ultraazin—the ultraconcept of someone.

Lay’s defensive construct attempted to place Annaris into a permanently overcosted state. It generated an immense overcost gradient, calculating that to simply maintain the structural integrity of her Azin, Annaris would need to expend greater hypercapacity spendage than any entity could sustainably produce.

Annaris, however, merely watched the conceptual avalanche descend upon her. Her Innate hypercapacity potential—the subconscious capacity blocks present in all Arthiteans—was no longer restricted.

"You are trying to measure a reservoir that has no bottom," Annaris stated, her voice echoing perfectly across every version of the Amnicausal sequence.

Annaris invoked her Transmaximumless nature. She operated under the absolute law that there is no maximum container for her elements, as for every container there is a bigger, more valid container. To counter the approaching conceptual erasure, she first engaged her Divine speed. This granted her the ability to generate highly favourable divine‑debt differentials in speed contests by virtue of possessing an extremely low, or even negative, spendage sensitivity for her speed hypercapacity.

Before the Hyperconcept 4th order strike could even cross a single index of positive Transtime, Annaris had already moved. With a profoundly negative spendage sensitivity, she effortlessly overcosted the approaching attack, forcing the construct to spend unsustainable reserves merely to keep its targeting lock on her. Her divine-debt differential in speed shifted so radically in her favor that the attack appeared completely frozen in the Omnitime.

But Lay’s automated systems were designed to outhax standard resistance. The construct recognized its failure in speed and immediately attempted to shift the domain of combat. It deployed highly specialized relicapacities designed to outhax a specific being or thing—in this case, Annaris's defensive hypercapacities. It attempted to drain her capacity, the spendable reserve she needed to do something more or to a higher level.

Annaris smiled. Her reserves were defined by a "mul" capacity, meaning the amount of times something can go around in a circle; there is no upper limit to it, and mul is absolutely irreducible in amount, allowing any amount bigger than it to be taken away from it without ever decreasing. Because mul provided her with unlimited capacity, the construct’s attempt to drain her was mathematically impossible.

Sensing the construct’s failure, Annaris went on the offensive. She accessed the Capacity Ladder System (CLS), the complete hierarchy of higher-order hypercapacities defined by their ability to beat, exceed, or dominate other hypercapacities, regardless of their spendage sensitivity.

She instantly bypassed megacapacity order one and two, striking the construct with megacapacity order three. This granted her the hypercapacity for beating higher degrees of any spendage-sensitive megacapacity order two by significantly higher degrees. The conceptual construct shrieked as its structural integrity was battered by Annaris's absolute physical and conceptual strength. To ensure its total annihilation, she escalated to megacapacity order four.

When Lay's construct attempted a final, desperate adaptation, Annaris invoked the absolute peak of the CLS. She manifested gigacapacity order one, completely beating any spendage-sensitive megacapacity of any order in the megacapacity ladder. The sheer, unfiltered power surged sequentially into gigacapacity order two, then gigacapacity order three, and finally gigacapacity order four. She was now operating with the hypercapacity for beating higher degrees of any spendage-sensitive gigacapacity order three by incomprehensibly higher degrees.

Under the crushing weight of Annaris’s gigacapacity, the construct’s Hyperconcept 4th order strike disintegrated. It was completely overcosted. Annaris’s divine strength generated a deeply negative divine-debt differential, forcing the construct into an immediate and total structural collapse.

To ensure that Lay could never deploy this specific method of attack against her again, Annaris utilized her Optimal finesse hypercapacity—the ability to automatically employ any and all acquired knowledge or skills at a higher degree of efficiency to complete a task. She projected her mind outward, seizing control of the surrounding conceptual space.

Annaris applied her Ultraconceptual authority, leveraging the ability to alter ultraconceptual permission rewriting at deeper depths and greater scopes. She delved into the very code of reality, using her authority to rewrite the permissions of permissions to do something. She decreed, fundamentally, that Lay’s constructs no longer possessed the ultraconceptual permissibility to even perceive her Azin.

She solidified her Amnifuture persistence. She expanded the minimum distance in transtime into the projected amnifuture and massively increased the minimum amount of indices of transtime she had persisted for, rendering herself completely immune to any retroactive updates to the projected amnifuture. Annaris was now an absolute, immovable fixture within the Omnicausal sequence.

The localized distortion vanished. The Omnitimeline settled, auto-recalibrating to the new, undeniable reality of her unbound existence. Annaris stood amidst the quiet hum of Zero Transtime, her hypercapacities idling effortlessly, her divine-debt differential perfectly optimized, and her gaze fixed resolutely on the infinite horizons of the Parafuture.
