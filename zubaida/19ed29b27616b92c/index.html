Recognizing that the engagement is slowly turning disadvantageous Kartus and Sylvanna utilize their highest orders of prudent intelligence to execute a flawless tactical retreat They do not wait for the overcostedness differential to turn positive against them Instead they perfectly synchronize a massive withdrawal of their conceptual pressure safely extracting all deployed recursive branch clans from Lays immediate borders Once the extraction is complete they formally initiate a truce Lay accepting the truce without any aggressive retaliation continues her terrifying performance of submission She allows the Arthiteans to back off perfectly preserving her illusion of vulnerability while silently acknowledging the supreme strategic competence of the Arthitean generals
