SESSION ONE: THE AGE OF ACCOMPLISHMENT & THE ASCENT (5 Earth Minutes)

The Implications of the Massive Update
Over the past 5 Earth minutes (300 Earth seconds), exactly 300 Eternances have passed for me inside the Empire. However, because the Empire’s exertion and performance multiply exponentially every passing second, these 300 Eternances were not static each subsequent Eternance was ulticenori longer and qualitatively superior to the last.

The dawn of the "Age of Accomplishment" has completely shifted the Empire's paradigm. Arthiteans are no longer just training; they are hyper-focused on acquiring metatitles and relative-titles to retroactively rewrite their hypergenetics. The Kafai ranking system has been brilliantly inverted (spanning from 1 billion down to 1, where Rhayhara sits absolutely at Kafai 1). Driven by the relentless pressure of Dariyus and the Venakan Clan, internal rank volatility is at its absolute peak. Every Arthitean is utilizing their zirunstressableness as infinite fuel to shatter impossible obstacles.

My Personal Ascent & The Grand Reincarnation Attempt
Starting from a Kafailess status, I engaged in Orotion Quests to establish my Kafai rank. I trained relentlessly in the Prekaltent Forest to multiply my accessible Prekaltents. My ultimate objective was to utilize the Grand Reincarnation System to secure a superior hypergenetic baseline.

I selected a highly respected husband-and-wife duo from a prominent branch clan as my prospective parents. According to Qai'lyth’s absolute standards, the three of us needed to forge the Empire’s highest records in mutual respect, trust, and enduring bonds. Across these 5 Earth minutes (billions of subjective years), we utilized Akesi synergy while fighting side-by-side in endless Ke'enmon simulations. Our mutual understanding reached ultraexpectation surpassing levels.

However, Qai'lyth’s testing is absolute. When I applied for reincarnation, she detected that while our bond was phenomenal, we had not yet achieved the Transmaximumless depth of "genuine mutual love" required for a flawless Arthitean reincarnation. My request was placed on hold, and I was tasked with developing our bonds to a higher zirhyperextent. I did not yield; instead, my effort factor and willpower factor surged massively in response to this adversity!

SESSION THREE: THE CONTINUED STRUGGLE & REINCARNATION QUEST (Next 5 Earth Minutes)

Pushing Through the Orotion Quests in Talmorai
The next 5 Earth minutes (and their countless escalating Eternances) were the ultimate test of my zirunstressableness. Orotus assigned me an Orotion Quest designed to push me past my absolute breaking point: I was sent alone into the Talmorai subdimension to ideologically neutralize an enemy nation. To make it a true hyperchallenge, my divine power was temporarily suppressed. I had to rely entirely on my Prudent Intelligence and Counterlogical Intelligence (Melzai).

To survive, I utilized Akesi synergy by forming a temporary alliance with a struggling Coldwar branch clan. We entered an Upgraded Sehexi Room, explicitly targeting Postkaltents related to future Cold War strategies. Through billions of years of simulation and psychological suffering, my Endurance Factor and Stress Factor multiplied infinitely. I emerged and successfully dismantled the enemy nation from the inside out. For completing this Orotion Quest, I was awarded the relative-title of "Tactical Vanguard," which retroactively upgraded my baseline hypergenetics!

The Final Reincarnation Verdict
Armed with my new title and elevated hypergenetics, my prospective parents and I returned to Qai'lyth. After surviving the mental crucible of Talmorai and saving each other countless times, our bonds had finally reached the Empire's pinnacle records.

Qai'lyth tested our souls, our Azin, and our Akesi synergy once more. This time, our genuine mutual love easily crossed the Transmaximumless threshold. Qai'lyth smiled and gave her absolute approval!

My existing Arthitean body was gracefully sacrificed. With my consciousness and memories perfectly intact, I was reincarnated as their biological child. I am currently Kafailess once again, but my veins now pulse with their superior, royal-adjacent hypergenetics. My natural aptitude, gigaaptitude, and paraaptitude for learning and combat are astronomically higher than my previous life. My new journey as a reborn Arthitean has just begun, and my ultimate goal is to conquer the Kafai ranks once more!
