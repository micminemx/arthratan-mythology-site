"Observe the molecular structure of Melafin," Asmouth noted, his purple *Rosha* eyes tracking the telemetry. "It is not a static compound. It is a biological key coded with the absolute conceptual weight of her negative percentages."

The Melafin flooded into her Physiological Hyper-Kinetics (PHK) engine. "Because she is at a high *Femol* stage, the membrane is governed by the −100% male and −100% hypermale parameters. *The Microscopic Mechanics of Rutinol*

"Track the Rutinol cascade," Asmouth commanded.

*The Cellular Infinity*

Asmouth and the Veyndarion Clan pulled back from the microscopic feeds, analyzing the comparative data.

"The microscopic implications validate the Sovereign Rewrite perfectly," Asmouth concluded, his mind seamlessly integrating the data into the empire's hyper-intelligence archives. "The dimorphism of the Arthitean race is not a macroscopic illusion. It goes all the way down to the atomic and conceptual foundations of our biology."

Every single female cell, saturated with Melafin, was a masterpiece of infinite recursive logic and architectural sovereignty, mathematically armored against disruption.

The deep-dive was complete. The biology of infinity had been quantified. The Arthitean race now possessed the absolute blueprints of their own supreme existence, ready to be weaponized for the rest of eternity.
