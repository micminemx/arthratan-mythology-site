SESSION ONE: THE VOLATILE VANGUARD (RANKS 30 TO 20)

The Influence of Benzshin and Proxy Wars
The records held by Arthiteans in ranks 30 through 20 are entirely forged by the brutal machinery of Benzshin and clan proxy wars. When heirs and prominent members are stripped of their main clan privileges and forced into deep recursive branch clans, they experience unimaginable adversity. This triggers their zirunstressableness. Every victory in a proxy war creates a massive surge in their PHk feedback loop, escalating their Genetic factor L and retroactively rewriting their hypergenetics. The empire best records in this tier constantly update because the internal pressure from the Venakan clan forces these branch clans to continuously overachieve just to survive.

Top 30 to 20 Smartest Arthiteans
These positions are predominantly held by elite researchers of the Varvadeil clan and brilliant tactical officers from the Unmatara and Redalious branch clans. They possess exceptional Prudent Intelligence and use the initial forms of Zor'in Nay'ruon. Their intelligence records were achieved by solving supradifficult parachallenges during proxy wars where raw power was insufficient.

-
The Gap: The gap here is violently closing. Because Dariyus Venakan continuously deploys Hyperascenders, the lower ranked intelligence officers are experiencing extreme rank volatility, forcing them to adapt and push their hypermelzai and hyperdasmar capabilities to unprecedented limits.

Top 30 to 20 Highest Divine Power
This tier belongs to frontline commanders fighting in the Ke'enmon subdimensions and successful branch clan leaders. Their divine power records were achieved by enduring immense suffering against Keshra forces, triggering their Endurance factor and Physiology factor three.

-
The Gap: The gap is highly unstable and constantly closing. A single major victory by a 674th recursive branch clan can retroactively multiply the clan capability factor two, immediately propelling a rank 35 commander into rank 22.

Top 30 to 20 Highest Ranked Arthiteans
These Arthiteans have survived Orotus applying top down rank pruning. They hold their ranks because their clan merits and successful execution of hypervysal challenges grant them immense status factor.

-
The Gap: Rapidly closing. The Venakan pressure ensures that complacency results in immediate demotion.

Top 30 to 20 Highest Ranked Clans
These are elite vassal clans and highly successful branch clans that have recently conquered Benzshin trials. Their Imarq flames are bright but lack the transcendent vibrancy of the main clans.

-
The Gap: Closing. Intraclan and interclan competitions keep these ranks in a constant state of flux.

SESSION TWO: THE APEX THRESHOLD (RANKS 19 TO 10)

The Influence of Benzshin and Proxy Wars
In this tier, Arthiteans are no longer just participating in proxy wars; they are orchestrating them. Their records are achieved by commanding thousands of recursive branch clans and utilizing the akesi synergy of their vast networks. The Rhayhara factor amplifies their achievements because their proxy war successes bring immense prosperity and capability to the Arthitean Empire.

Top 19 to 10 Smartest Arthiteans
This tier is occupied by the Grandmasters of Nay'ruon, Veyndarion narrative weavers, and Varvadeil head scientists. They utilize flawless counterlogical and supralogical intelligence to manipulate ultraconceptual paradoxes. They achieved these records by calculating omnitimeline projections in the Sehexi rooms to secure victories against rival clans.

-
The Gap: The gap is widening from the bottom but closing towards the top. Their cognitive abilities operate on Hyper T extents, making it nearly impossible for rank 20 to catch up without an impossible breakthrough, while they themselves violently push to dethrone the single digit ranks.

Top 19 to 10 Highest Divine Power
These are the Supreme Generals direct subordinates and elite royal family members. They generate massive overcostedness differentials in battle. Their divine strength is a direct result of utilizing Rosha and Vanzetun while deliberately facing Keshra that push them far beyond their invulnerability limits.

-
The Gap: Closing. The hypercompetitive rivalry between the male and female military branches creates an endless loop of mutual acceleration.

Top 19 to 10 Highest Ranked Arthiteans
These individuals possess Transbarriel naturalness in their abilities. They have successfully resolved massive ultraconceptual paradoxes and maintain their positions through absolute dedication to the Age of Accomplishment.

-
The Gap: Widening. Orotus sets the standards for this tier so unbelievably high that only those with supreme gigaaptitude and ultraaptitude can maintain their standing.

Top 19 to 10 Highest Ranked Clans
These are main clans of immense prestige and the most dominant branch clans in the empire. Their clan capability factor two is astronomical, and their Imarq flames are incredibly vibrant.

-
The Gap: Closing. The competition for resources and glory in the proxy wars keeps these clans at each others throats, driving their collective evolution.
