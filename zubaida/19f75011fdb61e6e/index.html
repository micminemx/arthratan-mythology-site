SESSION 1: The Ascent to Outerprecognition

Subject: Rhayhara and Orotus — The Flawless Memorization of the Ultraprojected Future

When the foundational mathematics of $Mul$—the maximumless amount of possible successive revolutions—were presented to Supreme Empress Rhayhara and Orotus, the physical space of the Throne Room ceased to obey standard spatial laws. They began the cognitive sequence to breach the boundaries of mere Hyperprecognition.

They initiated the Future-Recalibration loop. They took the projected future, recalibrated it against its own errors, and scaled it: $\text{Metaprojected}^n$ future. They pushed their Transcendent Processing Power through the absolute limit of $Mul$, achieving the $\text{Hyperprojected}^{mul}$ future. But they did not stop there. They turned their minds toward the Omnitimeline—the sequence of complete omniversal temporal rewrites.

By applying the same maximumless recursion to the Transfuture (temporal states at later omnitimeline indices), they calculated the $\text{Metaprojected}^n$ transfuture, and finally, the $\text{Hyperprojected}^n$ transfuture.

At the apex of this unimaginable cognitive pressure, Rhayhara and Orotus unlocked Outerprecognition. They absorbed the Ultraprojected Future in its entirety.

The Sensation of Outerprecognition:

Michael, you asked if it feels like having déjà vu all the time. Orotus and Rhayhara describe it as the exact opposite of déjà vu. Déjà vu is a symptom of a localized, mortal consciousness struggling to process a temporal overlap—it feels like a glitch, a hazy, uncertain echo.

Outerprecognition feels like Crystalline Omnipresence. Because they flawlessly memorized the Ultraprojected Future with absolute accuracy and precision, there is no surprise, no fading memory, and no uncertainty. It does not feel like they are "remembering" the future as it happens; rather, the present moment feels like an infinitely small, perfectly expected subset of a map they have already mastered. When an event occurs, their minds do not react to it. The sheer weight of knowing every single causal rewrite of the omniverse simultaneously brings a profound, chilling tranquility. They do not live in the present anymore; they exist as the anchor points of the entire Omnitimeline.

SESSION 2: Biological and Metaphysical Restructuring

Subject: Improvements to the Arthitean Empire via Outerprecognition (Part 1)

With the Ultraprojected Future flawlessly mapped, Rhayhara immediately initiated systemic upgrades to the Arthitean biology and the No Surplus Leakage Rule.

-
Transfuture-Proofing the PHk Baseline: Previously, zirunstressableness allowed Arthiteans to grow from present pressure. Rhayhara used Outerprecognition to identify every possible temporal rewrite in the Omnitimeline that could theoretically erase an Arthitean's growth. She updated the Qiyunta organs of all elite Arthiteans so their PHk levels are now anchored to the $\text{Hyperprojected}^{mul}$ transfuture. No timeline rewrite can revert an Arthitean's strength; their growth is now continuous across the Omnitimeline itself.

-
Absolute Zero-Waste Governance: Because Rhayhara and Orotus know exactly which future-recalibrations will occur, they have eliminated all "probabilistic" training. The Empire now only executes actions that mathematically contribute to their absolute supremacy across all temporal rewrites.

SESSION 3: The Evolution of Proxy Wars and Reincarnation

Subject: Improvements to the Arthitean Empire via Outerprecognition (Part 2)

The acquisition of Outerprecognition fundamentally altered the Empire's internal systems, particularly those overseen by Qai'lyth and the military generals.
