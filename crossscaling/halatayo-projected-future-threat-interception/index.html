Halatayo — Projected-Future Threat Interception

The source feat is strongest as a typed future-threat interception relation, not as a numerical infinity claim derived from Halatayo's title.

Title firewall: “Conqueror of the Infinite Parachallenge” is canonical title text. This record does not infer infinite distance, infinite AP, a completed infinite hierarchy, or conquest of every possible future from the epithet alone.

External benchmark

Define FTI-1 (Future-Threat Interception) as a completed case where a threat represented in a projected/forecast future is made directly actionable before the threatened state manifests, and the subject neutralizes the threat in that projected-future intervention context. Define FTI-R as FTI-1 plus an explicit retroactive aptitude improvement for the same class of future-facing challenge.

Absolute crossscale

VALID-LOWER-BOUND: Halatayo satisfies FTI-1 and FTI-R. The source states projected-Amnifuture access, destruction of the threatening hyperchallenge, and Genetic Factor 14's retroactive future-challenge aptitude effect.

Relative crossscale

VALID: after Genetic Factor 14, Halatayo's baseline ability to face challenges from the projected future is explicitly superior to his peers. This comparison is limited to the stated future-challenge aptitude; it does not establish omnistat superiority over those peers.

Metarelative crossscale

VALID-LOWER-BOUND: the relation changes from projected future threat → threatened present/future Clan Capability loss to projected threat → premanifest intervention → threat destruction → retroactively improved aptitude for projected-future challenges.

Hyperrelative crossscale

Define FITH (Future-Interception Totalizer) only over {projected-Amnifuture threat access, premanifest neutralization of the stated hyperchallenge, Genetic Factor 14 retroactive future-challenge aptitude}. Halatayo scales to FITH as representational compression of this sourceclosed event.

Verdict: VALID-LOWER-BOUND. FITH creates no extra future conquest and no new magnitude.

Bridgeproof

The external benchmark and native source concern the same property: acting upon and neutralizing a threat in a projected-future context before its threatened reduction of Clan Capability Factor Two manifests. The source then explicitly types the resulting retroactive improvement as aptitude for challenges from the projected future.

Assumptions and skeptical objections

  • Title Literalism Firewall: “Infinite Parachallenge” does not quantify the quest by itself.
  • Projected-future ontology firewall: a projected Amnifuture is kept as the source's own future-representation ontology; it is not automatically equated with every external time/future system.
  • Aptitude-Inheritance Nonidentity: Halatayo's lineage receives paraaptitude for predicting/dismantling future threats; descendants do not automatically inherit the exact magnitude of Halatayo's achieved quest feat.
  • Statdomain Firewall: future-threat interception does not transfer to raw AP, durability or generic speed without additional evidence.
  • Genetic Factor 14: the source establishes the retroactive baseline aptitude relation, not an arithmetic multiplier.

Verdict and FID

Overall verdict: VALID-LOWER-BOUND in projected-future threat interception and future-challenge aptitude; INVALID if inflated into infinite AP, conquest of every future, or descendant feat-magnitude identity.

FID sentence: Halatayo enters a projected Amnifuture, destroys a hyperchallenge threatening Clan Capability Factor Two with Divine Agility and Rosha, and gains a Genetic-Factor-14 rewrite making his baseline aptitude for projected-future challenges superior to peers.
