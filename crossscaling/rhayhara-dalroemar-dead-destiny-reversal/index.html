Rhayhara–Dalroemar Dead-Destiny Reversal

This record crossscales the five-session Dalroemar sequence by preserving the actual relation-changes in the source: a threat-condition defined as functionally unavoidable loss is first survived, then actively pushed back, then forced into genuine submission, then repeatedly defeated as training.

Canon boundary: Dead-Destiny Reversal Chain (DDRC) and Dalroemar Reversal Totalizer (DRTD) are external analytical shorthand. They are not native Arthratan powers, titles, objects or cosmological tiers.

Absolute crossscale

VALID-LOWER-BOUND: Rhayhara satisfies DDRC-4 for the exact progression above. The absolute result is outcome-control depth across a same-opponent escalation sequence, not a numerical size for “dead destiny.”

Relative crossscale

VALID: the source establishes a strict qualitative progression in Rhayhara's contest relation to Dalroemar: first-state Rhayhara achieves non-loss; later Rhayhara actively pushes back a more serious Dalroemar; later still she forces genuine submission; subsequent encounters become repeated victories. This licenses R3 contest-control > R2 contest-control > R1 contest-control on the Dalroemar matchup relation. It does not automatically rank every unrelated stat.

Metarelative crossscale

VALID: the strongest relation-over-relation structure is certain-loss condition → non-loss → causal-attribution rewrite → stronger rematch → pushback → higher rewrite → full adaptive contest → cost-collapse/submission → recurring training victories. The progression is evidentially stronger than reducing all sessions to “Rhayhara hit harder,” because the decisive predicate moves from survival to pressure-control to opponent-system sustainability.

Hyperrelative crossscale

Define DRTD as a single representational referent containing exactly the four proved DDRC rungs, the stated pararetroactive transitions between them, and the same-opponent continuity.

VALID-LOWER-BOUND: Rhayhara can be represented as operating across/completing DRTD because each component is directly sourced. DRTD compresses the sequence; it does not create an additional victory, a universal destiny-negation power or a larger infinity.

Exact bridgeproof

The external criterion and native evidence share the same property: change in outcome-control against a continuously identifiable opponent/condition. Session 2 establishes non-loss under the dead-destiny condition. Session 3 explicitly says Rhayhara actively pushes Dalroemar back. Session 4 says she forces genuine submission by making his adaptive growth unsustainable. Session 5 states repeated fully serious victories. No crossstat substitution is required.

Assumptions

  • “0% probability of survival,” “mathematical certainty” and “functionally impossible to not lose” are retained as native threat-language and used only to type the source's intended outcome-condition. They are not converted into an external probability theorem applying to every possible formal model.
  • The same Dalroemar identity persists through the repeated sequence as the source presents it.
  • Pararetroactive Bloodline Blessings preserve the named causal-attribution improvements; they do not automatically rewrite every unrelated physical statistic or external event.
  • The source's cost-based third-encounter victory supports overcostedness/resource-sustainability dominance in that contest, not a universal claim that Rhayhara's raw energy reserve exceeds Dalroemar's.

Strongest skeptical objections

  • Title Literalism: “Calamity to All” is an epithet, not a self-executing universal quantifier over every fictional or external entity.
  • Dead-destiny inflation: defeating a source-defined certain-loss condition proves reversal of that canon condition; it does not automatically equal author-level control, omnipotence, or superiority to every external fate system.
  • Open Recursion ≠ Completed Infinity: Session 5 says the ladder continues “recursively without limit.” This licenses a nonterminal recurrence rule, not completion of an actually infinite number of fights.
  • Statdomain: progression from survival to submission establishes stronger matchup/contest control. Exact speed, durability, intelligence and AP ratios remain unproved unless separately bridged.

Verdict and FID

Overall verdict: VALID-LOWER-BOUND. Strong for same-opponent outcome-reversal depth and pararetroactive progression; invalid if rewritten as a single untyped “infinite AP” tier.

FID sentence: Against Dalroemar, Rhayhara progresses from surviving a source-defined certain-loss state, to pushing back a more serious rematch, to forcing genuine submission by making his transadaptive growth unsustainable, then repeatedly defeating his fully serious state as training.
