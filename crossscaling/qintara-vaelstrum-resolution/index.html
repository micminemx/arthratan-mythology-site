Qintara–Vaelstrum Resolution

This record preserves four distinct event-relations from Qintara's completed Vaelstrum duel: probability/fate effect control, traversal, injury-triggered adaptation and dynamic overcostedness. It does not force them into one scalar “power level.”

Canon boundary: Zero-to-Certain Contact Counter (ZCC-1), Finite-Time Infinite-Distance Traversal (FTIDT) and the Qintara–Vaelstrum Resolution Totalizer are crossscale-only analytical constructs.

Native evidence

Vaelstrum's Probability-Erasure Field reduces Qintara's chance of landing a physical blow to zero and causes strikes to retroactively miss. Qintara's Axiomatic Fate Inversion changes the field's underlying relation so attacks are guaranteed to contact. The source then states that she moves with Divine-Agility and “crosses the infinite void between them in a microsecond.” A singularity wound is healed/adapted from in less than a nanosecond, and Qintara later defeats Vaelstrum through Divine-Strength/overcostedness pressure.

External benchmark definitions

ZCC-1: convert an adversarial mapping that makes contact probability zero / miss probability certain into a mapping that guarantees contact. FTIDT: traverse a source-explicit infinite separation in a source-explicit finite nonzero time, where the source does not state teleportation, distance-shortening or a finite replacement path.

Absolute crossscale

ZCC-1: VALID. The source directly states both endpoints of the relation. FTIDT: VALID-CONDITIONAL. It is satisfied on the literal metric reading of “infinite void” and “crosses … in a microsecond.”

Relative crossscale

ZCC-1: Qintara's counterrelation defeats Vaelstrum's demonstrated guaranteed-miss architecture. FTIDT: under matching path-traversal semantics, the event exceeds an external one-microsecond movement benchmark whose traversable distance remains finite. This result is movement-domain only; it does not automatically set reaction, cognition or attack speed.

Metarelative crossscale

attempted strike → retroactive miss is transformed into attempted strike → guaranteed contact. Separately, infinite separation → inaccessible under finite-distance traversal is source-stated as infinite separation → crossed within 1 μs. These are distinct relation-shifts.

Hyperrelative crossscale

Define the Qintara–Vaelstrum Resolution Totalizer as exactly {ZCC-1, FTIDT, sub-nanosecond recovery/adaptation, dynamic overcostedness defeat}. VALID-LOWER-BOUND as representational compression; it neither merges the stats nor creates an additional feat.

Assumptions and strongest skeptical objection

  • The FTIDT bridge assumes the source's “infinite void” is literal/metric rather than merely hyperbolic or an untyped fictional descriptor.
  • The source names traversal (“crosses”) and Divine-Agility but does not explicitly rule out every exotic movement mechanism; no such bypass is stated, so the result remains conditional rather than absolute.
  • Vaelstrum's ability to digest timelines of entire multiverses is a capability statement. It does not automatically establish passive durability of equal scope.
  • The source's post-injury performance language is preserved as a local comparative claim, not converted into an external arithmetic infinity.

Verdict and FID

Overall verdict: VALID for ZCC-1 and the completed battle chain; VALID-CONDITIONAL for FTIDT; INVALID for automatic multiversal-durability transfer from Vaelstrum's timeline-digestion capability.

FID sentence: Qintara converts Vaelstrum's guaranteed-miss probability field into guaranteed contact, crosses the source-explicit infinite separation in one microsecond on the literal traversal reading, adapts from a singularity wound and breaks the opponent through overcostedness pressure.
