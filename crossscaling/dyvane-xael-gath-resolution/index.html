Native evidence

Xael-Gath bypasses physical distance and attempts to sever the causal link between Dyvane's brain and muscles. Dyvane's Conceptual Reflection Mastery directly targets the production and persistence of that Hax, invalidating, inverting and reflecting it. Xael-Gath then blocks a punch with an explicitly infinite defensive Hypercapacity reserve, but Dyvane imposes an unsustainable Transcapacity requirement and the barrier collapses. A landed anti-logic wound triggers rapid PHK/zirunstressableness recovery and permanent adaptation before the terminal Venshen-linked decapitation.

External benchmark definitions

EPC-1: the defender directly targets the production or persistence relation that generates/maintains an adversarial effect, rather than merely tolerating its output. DCC: a contest comparator based on whether a participant can sustainably operate at the required rate/Lydrextent under dynamically imposed pressure, independently of total stored reserve.

Absolute crossscale

VALID: Dyvane satisfies EPC-1 against Xael-Gath's causal severance because the source explicitly names production and persistence as the Superhax target. VALID-LOWER-BOUND: he also wins the event-specific DCC interaction against the defensive barrier.

Relative crossscale

VALID: Dyvane's demonstrated counterrelation is superior to Xael-Gath's demonstrated causal-severance relation in this event; his contest pressure also exceeds Xael-Gath's sustainable defensive Transcapacity requirement at the decisive contact. No exact AP ratio follows.

severance → motor failure becomes severance production/persistence → inversion/reflection → hostile effect failure. Separately, infinite reserve → defensive persistence fails because required sustainable higher-order operation > opponent's sustainable bandwidth. The relation-shift is sourcecomplete without treating infinity as a conventional number.

Hyperrelative crossscale

Define the Dyvane–Xael-Gath Resolution Totalizer as exactly {EPC-1 counter, DCC barrier win, injury-triggered permanent adaptation, terminal projected-future defeat}. VALID-LOWER-BOUND as proof compression only. It creates no fifth feat and cannot be exported to an unrelated opponent without same-property bridges.

Assumptions and strongest skeptical objection

  • “Infinite reserve” is preserved as source wording, but reserve magnitude and sustainable Transcapacity bandwidth are distinct predicates in current Divinity doctrine.
  • The shockwave erasing millions of projected enemy fleets is collateral breadth; fleet durability is not numerically specified.
  • The source-relative “infinitely stronger than a millisecond prior” wording supports a pre/post selfrelation but is not silently converted into an external cardinal multiplier.

Verdict and FID

Overall verdict: VALID-LOWER-BOUND. Strong for typed effect-countering and resource-sustainability dominance; INVALID if rewritten as “Dyvane raw AP is numerically greater than every infinity.”

FID sentence: Dyvane defeats Xael-Gath by reflecting a causal-severance Hax at its production/persistence layer, breaking an infinite-reserve defense through unsustainable Transcapacity pressure, adapting from a landed anti-logic wound and completing the kill.
