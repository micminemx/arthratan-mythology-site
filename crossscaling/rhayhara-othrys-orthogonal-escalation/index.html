Rhayhara–Othrys Orthogonal Escalation Chain

This record externally crossscales the five completed Othrys encounters preserved in Zubaida transmission 19f0d1c0f4a5d5e5. Its purpose is relationprecision: each encounter attacks a different dependency, so the strongest defensible result is a typed escalation-chain rather than a single numerical power tier.

Canon boundary: Orthogonal Escalation Chain (OEC) and Othrys Escalation Totalizer (OETO) are analytical shorthand invented for crossscaling. They are not native Arthratan powers, objects or canonical terminology.

External benchmark definition

Define OEC-k as a sequence of k completed hostile conditions where each successor challenge attacks a materially different prerequisite or relation from the challenge immediately before it, and the subject reaches a source-established non-loss/closure in every included condition. OEC measures orthogonal counterdomain closure, not energy magnitude.

Absolute crossscale

VALID-LOWER-BOUND: Rhayhara satisfies OEC-5 in the exact five-domain sequence above. The absolute result is breadth/dependency-depth: five distinct failure-relations were closed in completed encounters.

Relative crossscale

VALID-LOWER-BOUND: after each pararetroactive Blessing, Othrys must recalibrate because the preceding insufficiency has been closed. Therefore each post-closure Rhayhara configuration is source-relationally superior to its own prior configuration on the particular defeated challenge-domain. No exact universal multiplier or AP ratio is given.

Metarelative crossscale

VALID: the repeated relation is identified insufficiency → Othrys condition → Rhayhara non-loss → pararetroactive closure → orthogonally retyped insufficiency. That relation-over-relation structure is stronger evidence than merely saying later stages “hit harder,” because the target predicate itself changes from evasion to self-integrity to environmental permission to acquisition-legality to ontology.

Hyperrelative crossscale

Define OETO as a single external representational referent containing exactly the five proved OEC relations and their successor/recalibration links. Rhayhara can be represented as completing/operating across OETO because every included component has a source anchor.

Verdict: VALID-LOWER-BOUND. This is compression of H1–H5, not creation of a sixth feat, a completed infinite ladder, or superiority to every conceivable external threat.

Bridgeproof

The benchmark and native evidence share the same property: each OEC rung asks whether a subject reaches non-loss when the next adversarial condition targets a different prerequisite from the prior condition. The source explicitly states that Othrys recalibrates because a newly pararetroactive Blessing closes the previous gap, then names the successor attack and its different target. No crossstat substitution is required.

Assumptions and skeptical counterchecks

  • The source's phrases “geometrically massive,” “infinite gap,” “absolute maximum ceiling” and “infinite ladder” are preserved as native wording but are not converted into a cardinal, real multiplier, Toren value, Apossible value, spatial dimension or raw-AP tier.
  • B4's “relevant blessing for any unanticipated challenge” is not exported as omnipotence or automatic victory in arbitrary foreign systems. The same source immediately presents H5 as a further challenge, which itself demonstrates why the quantifier must stay canon-domain contextual.
  • H5's rewriting of the definition of a “foe” is an in-universe ontological operation. It is not author-over-fiction control.
  • The Session-7 statement that a future H6 would need to threaten the fundamental Omnitimeline framework is prospective system implication, not a completed H6 feat.
  • Pararetroactivity supports continuity of the named Blessing relation inside the transmission; it does not automatically transfer every historical physical magnitude into Present-State Stats.

Verdict and FID compression

Overall verdict: VALID-LOWER-BOUND. Strong for multidomain adaptive relation-depth; invalid if rewritten as one scalar infinity/AP hierarchy.

FID sentence: Across five completed Othrys Hypervysals, Rhayhara closed five different failure-relations—outercausal evasion, Azin rewrite, environmental power permissibility, Blessing-acquisition legality and ontological foe-definition—through source-stated pararetroactive Bloodline Blessing rewrites.
