# IA publication gates

Merge is allowed only after all of the following are evidenced on the final candidate SHA:

1. Semantic IA / OmniIndex V2 passes.
2. Publication Quality Gate passes.
3. Changed-file review finds no unintended canon, source, identity, or crossscaling semantic edits.
4. Generated discovery surfaces and sitemap contain valid canonical production routes only.
5. Candidate remains based on current `main` with no unreviewed upstream drift.

After merge, monitor post-merge generators and publication workflows, then independently verify the live site before calling the repair LIVE-VERIFIED.
