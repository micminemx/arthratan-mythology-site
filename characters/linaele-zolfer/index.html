Linaele Zolfer

Character Entity · Imperial / Zolfer Clan

The explicitly named leader of the Zolfer Clan, a Top-50-clan organization whose mandate is to engineer performance-amplifying artifacts that weaponize hostile entities against other evil entities while also generating challenge-pressure for Arthiteans.
