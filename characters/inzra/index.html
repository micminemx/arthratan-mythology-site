Inzra

Character Entity · Antagonist / Medonite

An apex Medonite antagonist whose defining power is Technogenesis: directly manifesting technology she can coherently map, enabling instant technotactical battlefield reconfiguration against the Arthitean Empire.

Appearance

Personality

  • Cold, engineered elegance; highly intelligent and technotactically improvisational.
  • Objectives evolve across canon from direct antagonism toward capture/study/enslavement-oriented uses of Arthitean power.

  • Risvulative-technokinetic improvisation, hax/counterprep and extreme multitasking in later character specifications.
  • Inzratic technology and resistance/hax scaling across First/Second/Third Orders.
