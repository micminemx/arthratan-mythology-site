Orotus

Divine Beast / Imperial-associated

A divine beast appearing as a holy black phoenix. Orotus is deeply embedded in Arthitean progression and ranking but is explicitly not an Arthitean.

Role
Rank arbiter; Orotion Quest granter; supreme intelligence figure
Species / state
Divine Beast — holy black phoenix
Sex / gender
Unspecified in retrieved email canon
Allegiance
Arthitean Empire — allied/embedded divine beast
Status
Active / not contradicted by retrieved canon

Titles

  • Supreme Rank Arbiter
  • Orotion Quest Granter
  • Smartest figure in several Arthitean ranking lists

Appearance

  • Canonical form: badassly holy black phoenix.
  • Standalone Orotus canon model is authoritative.

Personality

  • Extremely discerning and evaluative; constructs personalized, brutally scaled Orotion Quests.
  • Operates as a non-clan arbiter rather than an ordinary imperial rank-seeker.

Abilities

  • Generates effectively unlimited personalized Orotion Quests.
  • Regulates Kafai/Mefuli ranking structures and top-down rank pressure.
  • Transmaximumless prudent intelligence in ranking accounts.
  • Quest completion can trigger titles/metatitles whose genetic-factor consequences retroactively alter recipients and bloodlines.

Feats

  • Designed quests for Rhayhara, Qai'lyth, Dyvane, Kartus, Thalyros, Dariyus and Halatayo in the retrieved Orotion chronicle.
  • Serves as supreme rank manager while remaining outside Arthitean species classification.

Relationships

  • Rhayhara: supreme ruler whose empire Orotus helps pressure and rank.
  • Qai'lyth: complementary authority around Grand Reincarnation and relational validation.
  • Top clans: recipients of Orotion Quest pressure.

Information not yet established

  • Origin and creation history.
  • Whether Orotus has a fixed gender/sex classification.
  • Full ability taxonomy outside quest/rank functions.
