Rhayhara

Core / Imperial / Royal

Appearance

  • Canonical core model exists in the character-model directory.
  • General Arthitean phenotype canon: reddish-brown skin, dark-red hair, purple eyes, black phoenix-like wings.

Personality

  • Protector-ruler in later canon, but with a history of severe, high-pressure rule and war-goddess conduct.
  • Strongly achievement-driven and willing to alter the empire's past to normalize later improvements.

Abilities

  • Retroactive omnitimeline rewriting and canon-integrative time rewrite.
  • Negative Rewrite and later higher anticausal/causal-rewrite developments.
  • Divine power, Rosha and higher-order Arthitean scaling.
  • Royal-factor/bloodline effects that raise descendants and, through imperial systems, influence wider Arthitean baselines.
  • Capacity to authorize or rewrite governing laws/permissions in later stories.

Feats

  • Absolute Kafai Rank 1 in retrieved rankings.
  • Repeatedly identified as the strongest Arthitean and the empire's unreachable supreme benchmark.
  • Authored/established conditions allowing advanced states such as Venshen and Rosha to coexist without paradox in one Orotion account.

History

  • Rules the Arthitean Empire across the living canon and stands at the center of its retroactive historical revisions.
  • Establishes or authorizes repeated empirewide rewrites so newly added systems have always existed across Arthitean parahistory.
  • Her later character material distinguishes present protector-rulership from the destructive pressure and warlike legacies of earlier phases.

Relationships

  • Orotus functions as rank arbiter and high-intelligence counterpart.
  • Asmouth and the Varvadeil scientific apparatus frequently brief or advise her.
  • Dyvane and Qintara are her supreme military generals.
  • Qai'lyth administers Grand Reincarnation under the empire's order.
