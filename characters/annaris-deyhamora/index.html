Annaris

Character Entity · Antagonist / Nüreen-associated

A major disaster-class adversary specialized in long-horizon cold-war manipulation, contingency and preplanned error. Later email canon explicitly lists Annaris and Deyhamora as separate members of the Seven Disasters, so the former compound rendering is not treated as one identity.
