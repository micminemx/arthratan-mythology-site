225. Hyperfemale versus Ultrafemale 
A Hyperfemale can progress through active Hyperomega axes and recursively higher dimensional strata 
while remaining categorically Hyperfemale. After the complete adopted Hyperfemale dimensional domain is 
saturated, UF1 is constructed by SIH_dim as the third named category. 
A possible Ultrafemale novelty is not simply a new trait but a meta-dimensional constraint relation: two whole 
Hyper-level phenotype systems that lower Hyperfemale architecture can express only in mutually exclusive 
ways could become jointly coordinated. That can generate new whole-body anatomy without requiring any 
one local trait to monotonically increase. 
226. Ultrafemale versus Apexfemale and visual phenotype 
Ultrafemale and Apexfemale remain fully within the domain of physical phenotype. After the complete Ultra 
dimensional domain is saturated, AF1 is constructed by SIH_dim. Either category can have visibly apparent 
female anatomy because Gamma governs gross/outward phenotype as well as microscopic and 
physiological organization. 
A possible Ultrafemale manifestation is a visibly obvious whole-body morphology produced by cross-scale 
constraints coordinating skeleton, soft tissue, skin, reproductive anatomy, vascular distribution, and 
endocrine timing. A possible Apexfemale manifestation may place an additional meta-dimensional constraint 
over complete Ultra rule families. Either may instead be externally familiar while differing extraordinarily in 
development, regeneration, maturation, provenance, and counterfactual response. 
Both are compatible with the formal architecture until a specific phenotype law selects among them. 
227. Major Stage 2 Archwomanhood 
Suppose an Archfemale completes all finite-indexed minor hypercategories of Major Stage 1 and crosses the 
minor-category limit into Major Stage 2. 
ω
The new major envelope can modify the rules by which minor-category rule systems themselves operate. 
One possible biological consequence is that phenotype traits no longer behave as merely local outputs of 
one minor category but as coordinated outputs of a major-stage rule network that constrains entire 
sequences of future minor-category transformations. 
Visually, the woman may display anatomy impossible under any Major Stage 1 realization. Alternatively, 
some visible traits may overlap while the developmental counterfactual structure is radically different. Both 
possibilities are consistent with the formal distinction between phenotype appearance and phenotype rule 
architecture. 
228. Male worked mirror 
Replace Female with Male, SF with SM, HF with HM, AF with AM, UF with UM, Archwomanhood with 
Archmanhood, and female phenotype domains with their male analogues. The same deductions and 
non-deductions hold. 
A Supramale may have stage-specific gross male anatomy without being Hypermale. After the Supramale 
axis is Hyperomega-saturated, HM1 is strictly constructed by SIH rather than identified with the SM endpoint. 
UM1 follows completion of the Hypermale dimensional domain; AM1 follows completion of the Ultramale 
dimensional domain. Later categories and major stages follow the same typed address logic.
