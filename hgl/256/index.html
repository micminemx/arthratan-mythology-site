363. Logical deductions from the corrected SF/SM ladder 
D-SF-1: SF2 entails SF1 by IH projection, and entails NOT SM1 by projection of IH(NOT SM1). 
D-SF-2: SF2 entails PH(SF1) and NOT PH(SM1). It does not entail PH(NOT SM1). 
D-SF-3: SF2 entails UD(SF1) and NOT FD(SF1); it does not entail UD(NOT SM1). 
D-SF-4: SF3 entails SF2, NOT SM2, PH(SF2), NOT PH(SM2), UD(SF2), and NOT FD(SF2). 
D-SF-5: By recursive IH projection, every successor above SF2 entails its entire same-side predecessor 
chain, subject to limit closure at limits. 
D-SF-6: Female/male structural symmetry is preserved even though opposite-profile possession is negated 
rather than replaced by a nonstate PH source. 
D-SF-7: The old nine-conjunct and seven-conjunct formulas are not equivalent to the new eight/six formulas 
because they asserted additional positive UD and different PH content. 
364. Logical deductions from the strengthened Hyper recurrence 
D-HF-1: HF2 entails SIH_axis(HF1) and therefore every IH source licensed by the selected HF1 axis-tail. 
D-HF-2: HF2 entails SIH_axis(NOT HM1) and therefore every corresponding inherited structured non-HM 
source on that axis-tail. 
D-HF-3: HF2 entails PH(HF1) and NOT PH(HM1); neither is merely an SIH projection. 
D-HF-4: HF2 entails UD(HF1), but does not entail NOT FD(HF1) unless a separate rule is added. 
D-HF-5: HF3 has the same five semantic roles one dimensional order higher. 
D-HF-6: DimCount(HF_n)=n+1 for finite n remains unchanged by adding PH/NOT-PH/UD conjuncts because 
dimensional count is determined by coordinate construction, not number of formula conjuncts. 
D-HF-7: HF2 remains distinct from HF<1>[2,Omega]; strengthening its constitutive formula makes the 
nonidentity even more pronounced.
