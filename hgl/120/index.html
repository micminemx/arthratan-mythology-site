231. PA irreducibility still blocks decomposition 
PA(A AND B) ≠ PA(A) AND PA(B), in general. 
PA(A OR B) ≠ PA(A) OR PA(B), in general. 
PA(NOT A) ≠ NOT PA(A), in general. 
The metaexpressive reading strengthens rather than weakens these laws. If the entire inner logical function 
configures one developmental causal basis, decomposing it can erase the interaction rule that makes the 
basis what it is. 
232. Two different metaexpressive layers 
PA and Γ are both metaexpressively important, but they do not operate at the same type. 
PA metaexpression: developmental-causal. It concerns the causal logical basis under which 
anatomy/physiology is necessarily developable. 
Γ metaexpression: phenotype-regulatory. It concerns the stage-global rules under which concrete physical 
phenotype is expressible. 
developmental causal condition --PA--> developmental architecture --Γ_n--> admissible phenotype 
expression-space. 
This sequence is schematic rather than a claim that every phenotype is a deterministic one-step output of 
PA. The important point is type order: developmental causal structure and phenotype metaexpression are 
distinct causal/logical layers. 
233. Stage-relative phenotype modality 
Modal logic becomes natural once Γ is understood as a rule-system over phenotype-space. HGL therefore 
defines stage-relative biological necessity and possibility. 
□^F_n φ := φ holds in every phenotype realization permitted by Γ^F_n. 
◇^F_n φ := φ holds in at least one phenotype realization permitted by Γ^F_n. 
□^M_n φ := φ holds in every phenotype realization permitted by Γ^M_n. 
◇^M_n φ := φ holds in at least one phenotype realization permitted by Γ^M_n. 
These are not claims about unrestricted logical, metaphysical, or omniversal possibility. They are typed 
claims about biological phenotype expressibility under one current Archstage rule-regime. 
234. Required, permitted, and forbidden phenotype statuses 
For a phenotype proposition p at Stage n, Γ can assign at least three basic modal statuses. 
Required: □^F_n p 
Permitted: ◇^F_n p AND NOT □^F_n p
