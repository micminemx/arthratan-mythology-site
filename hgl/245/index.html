339. Archmanhood/manhood mirror 
Everything above has an exact male structural mirror: SM, HM, UM, AM; SIH_axis(SM1), SIH_axis(NOT 
SF1); HM_(n+1)=SIH_axis(HM_n) AND SIH_axis(NOT HF_n); UM1=SIH_dim(HM-domain) AND 
SIH_dim(NOT HF-domain); AM1=SIH_dim(UM-domain) AND SIH_dim(NOT UF-domain). Ordinary manhood 
remains distinct from Archmanhood just as ordinary womanhood remains distinct from Archwomanhood. 
Possible male phenotype domains include chest/torso, skeletal proportions, reproductive anatomy, face, 
muscle/fat distribution, skin/hair, vascular/endocrine physiology, microarchitecture, repair, and whole-body 
coordination. Higher SIH dimensionality does not deductively mean larger muscles, greater height, higher 
testosterone, or one stereotypically more masculine visible metric. 
340. Diagnostic observability and stage opacity 
The architecture strengthens the distinction between what a body is and what casual observation can read. A 
high-dimensional state can share current outward traits with a far lower state because Gamma noninjectivity 
remains permitted. Therefore visual observation alone need not determine dimensional order, minor 
category, or full Archaddress. 
HI, not D: an advanced Arthitean science would plausibly rely on provenance-sensitive assays, 
perturbation/provocation tests, longitudinal developmental responses, multiscale imaging, physiology, 
lineage records, or direct detection of SIH/Gamma structure rather than appearance alone. This is a 
worldbuilding inference, not a theorem that every institution must use one particular method.

D-SIH-2: SIH_axis(NOT X[a]) entails IH(NOT X[beta]) for every beta in the typed tail.

D-SIH-9: Current named category order is Supra -> Hyper -> Ultra -> Apex. 
D-SIH-10: Same source-domain cardinality does not entail same dimensional order.
