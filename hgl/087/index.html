138. The revised Stage-2 meanings 
The canonical female Stage 2 is 
SF2 = IH(SF1) AND IH(NOT SM1) AND PH(SF1) AND NOT PH(SM1) AND UD(SF1) AND PA(x) AND 
PA(NOT y) AND NOT FD(SF1) 
The canonical male Stage 2 is 
SM2 = IH(SM1) AND IH(NOT SF1) AND PH(SM1) AND NOT PH(SF1) AND UD(SM1) AND PA(NOT x) AND 
PA(y) AND NOT FD(SM1) 
These are not merely longer ways to write Stage 1. Stage 2 explicitly distinguishes inherited Stage-1 content, 
standalone primitive PA routes, inherited opposite-side structured-nonstate content, complete possession of 
the preceding same-side phenotype-profile, explicit non-possession of the complete opposite predecessor 
phenotype-profile, same-side whole-form Unflaw status, and same-side local nonflawhood. IH, PH/NOT-PH, 
UD, and NOT-FD are different logical types.
