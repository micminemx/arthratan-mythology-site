36. Why IH¬SMn is not merely “female” 
Even though a high Suprafemale stage is female-typed, the source expression 
¬SMn 
does not equal SFn and does not equal primitive Female. A huge number of states can be non-SMn without 
being Suprafemale at all. What makes the term specifically part of Archwomanhood is its placement inside 
the canonical female recursion: 
SF^(n+1) = IH(SF^n) AND IH(NOT SM^n) AND PH(SF^n) AND NOT PH(SM^n) AND UD(SF^n) AND NOT 
FD(SF^n), for n >= 2 
Therefore it is a mistake to write 
¬SMn=SFn. 
It is also a mistake to write 
IH¬SMn=SFn+1 
because the next Suprafemale stage also inherits its own previous female-side stage. 
37. Boolean redundancy does not imply inheritance redundancy 
Suppose 
SM3⇒SM2⇒SM1. 
Then contraposition gives 
¬SM1⇒¬SM2⇒¬SM3. 
Hence the bare logical statement ¬SM3 may be weaker than a previously established ¬SM1. If HGL were 
only a Boolean theory, this would threaten saturation. 
But the higher state does not merely add ¬SM3; it adds 
IH¬SM3, 
whose source is specifically ¬SM3 and whose inheritance layer is new. The state therefore contains a 
different genealogical causal object even when one projected truth condition is already implied.
