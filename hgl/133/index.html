277. Cross-layer modal bridge rules require explicit causality 
A developmental possibility does not automatically become a phenotype possibility. HGL therefore requires 
explicit bridge rules between PA-developmental modality and Γ-phenotype modality. 
◇^D_φ q does not automatically imply ◇^F_n phenotype(q). 
If a causal law C states that developmental realization q under Γ^F_n produces phenotype p, then the bridge 
can support a causal inference or deduction depending on the strength of C. 
278. Metaexpressive antithesis and NOT scope 
The new layer preserves HGL’s central lesson: the position of NOT matters. For a phenotype proposition p: 
NOT □_n p ≠ □_n NOT p. 
NOT ◇_n p ↔ □_n NOT p, under modal duality. 
PH(NOT S) ≠ NOT PH(S). 
PA(NOT φ) ≠ NOT PA(φ). 
IH(NOT S) ≠ NOT IH(S). 
These distinctions must be preserved simultaneously. HGL is now a multi-level scope logic: NOT can occur 
inside PA conditions, outside PA, inside IH sources, outside IH, inside PH sources, outside PH, and inside or 
outside modal operators. Each location has a different meaning. 
279. Final canonical rules of the metaexpression-modal revision 
Rule M1 — Ordinary phenotype is a physical biological expression; it need not be whole-body. 
Rule M2 — The Archwomanhood/Archmanhood phenotype proper is the global stage meta-phenotype Γ. 
Rule M3 — Global Γ means global governance of expression, not identical phenotype everywhere. 
Rule M4 — PH and Γ are noncollapsible. 
Rule M5 — PA and Γ are noncollapsible; they operate at different metaexpressive levels. 
Rule M6 — Nested Boolean/logical functions inside PA are allowed; PA(PA(...)) remains excluded from core 
HGL. 
Rule M7 — □/◇ are stage-relative biological modalities generated by Γ, not unrestricted metaphysical 
modalities. 
Rule M8 — Actual valid expression implies stage-relative possibility. 
Rule M9 — Stage-relative necessity implies actuality in a valid current realization. 
Rule M10 — Stage-relative impossibility excludes actuality in a valid current realization. 
Rule M11 — Higher stage need not be a subset/superset transformation of lower phenotype-space. 
Rule M12 — Stage stagnation fixes Γ but need not fix ordinary biological inputs or actual phenotype. 
Rule M13 — New womanhood/manhood phenotypes can emerge under static Γ without stage advancement. 
Rule M14 — A higher stage can introduce new phenotype dimensions, relations, conditional rules, or modal 
statuses. 
Rule M15 — No exact concrete phenotype is a deduction unless PH membership, Γ rule, or explicit causal 
law supplies it. 
These rules are authoritative over older phenotype wording wherever there is tension.
