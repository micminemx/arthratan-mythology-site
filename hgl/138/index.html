238. Macroanatomy is a first-class output of biological metaexpression 
Because Γ governs how female or male phenotype can or cannot express, gross anatomy is not a peripheral 
downstream possibility. It is one of the central output domains of the function. A stage can constrain 
geometry, proportions, relational anatomy, developmental timing, tissue composition, organ-system 
coordination, visible contour, and other macroscopically expressed variables. These examples identify 
classes of output, not exact canonized shapes. 
Female-side illustrative domains: breast morphology; gross reproductive anatomy; external genital 
morphology; pelvic/skeletal geometry; body contour; facial morphology; skin and hair appearance; gross 
muscular and adipose distribution; organ relationships. 
Male-side illustrative domains: chest/pectoral morphology; gross reproductive anatomy; external genital 
morphology; pelvic/skeletal geometry; facial morphology; body contour; skin/hair patterning; gross muscular 
and adipose distribution; organ relationships. 
Both sides: the stage can govern combinations and compatibility relations among traits, not merely isolated 
traits. 
239. Modal status of macroscopic phenotype 
The modal extension now applies explicitly to visible and gross-anatomical traits. Let p be a macroscopic or 
outward phenotype proposition. Then □^F_n p means p is true in every Γ^F_n-valid phenotype realization; 
◇^F_n p means at least one valid realization permits p; NOT ◇^F_n p means p cannot occur in a valid 
Stage-n Archfemale realization. Male notation mirrors this. 
If □^F_n p and the subject is a valid SF^n realization, then p is actual. [D] 
If NOT ◇^F_n p, then p is not actual in any valid SF^n realization. [D] 
If p is actually expressed by a valid SF^n subject, then ◇^F_n p. [D] 
If ◇^F_n p, p need not be actual in this particular subject. [D: countermodel available] 
If □^F_n(p OR q), one may not infer □^F_n p or □^F_n q. [D] 
The Macroscopic Manifestation Axiom supplies a necessary disjunction of stage-governed macro/outward 
alternatives, not necessarily one universal exact outward form. [D] 
240. A stronger interpretation of stage progression 
An Archwomanhood or Archmanhood transition changes more than a list of internal facts. It changes a global 
biological metaexpression regime whose outputs include the visible body. Therefore progression can change 
the rules by which outward and gross-anatomical phenotypes are permitted, required, forbidden, coupled, or 
generated. A higher stage can transform the modal topology of macroscopic phenotype-space. 
Possibility opening: NOT ◇^F_n p and ◇^F_(n+1) p. 
Necessitation: ◇^F_n p and □^F_(n+1) p. 
Prohibition: ◇^F_n p and NOT ◇^F_(n+1) p. 
Compatibility opening: ◇^F_n p AND ◇^F_n q but NOT ◇^F_n(p AND q), followed by ◇^F_(n+1)(p AND 
q). 
Conditionalization: p becomes expressible only when puberty/maturity/history variable d obtains under 
Γ^F_(n+1).
