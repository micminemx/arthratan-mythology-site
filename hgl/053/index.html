Q16. Why allow nested IH but not nested PA? 
Because PA’s spoken meaning is development due to an inner developmental condition, whereas IH is 
specifically an outer state-to-state inheritance operator. Recursive genealogy belongs to IH. 
Q17. What is canonical SF2?

PA(NOT y) AND NOT FD(SF1)

SM2 = IH(SM1) AND IH(NOT SF1) AND PH(SM1) AND NOT PH(SF1) AND UD(SM1) AND PA(NOT x) AND

Q22. Does a higher SF stage imply lower SF stages? 
Yes, by repeated IH projection. 
Q23. Does a higher SF stage imply NOT lower SM stages? 
Yes as a Boolean projection, through the inherited non-SM terms and cumulative stage relations. 
Q24. Why does that not make later non-SM inheritance redundant? 
Because IH preserves the exact source stage and inheritance depth. Boolean projection is not full HGL state 
identity. 
Q25. Is that “just metadata”? 
No within HGL. Provenance is explicitly part of the biological developmental state and may be read by the 
causal phenotype map. If a future version declared provenance biologically irrelevant, the ladder would need 
reevaluation.
