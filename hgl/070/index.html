103. Orthogonal does not mean causally unrelated 
When this document calls stage and maturity “distinct axes,” it means they are not definitionally identical. It 
does not mean they can never influence each other.

1.​

2.​
One-way coupled: stage influences maturation or maturation influences stage. 
3.​
Two-way coupled: each can alter the development of the other.
