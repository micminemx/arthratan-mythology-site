42. Ordinary womanhood 
Ordinary womanhood is not a single stereotyped phenotype. In HGL it is the female developmental domain 
before one specifies higher Suprafemale inheritance architecture. An ordinary woman can mature, age, 
reproduce or not reproduce, vary anatomically, vary physiologically, and undergo substantial development 
without ever changing an Archwomanhood stage. 
Therefore the comparison “ordinary woman versus Archfemale” is not “undeveloped woman versus 
developed woman.” It is ordinary female developmental organization versus female developmental 
organization carrying additional Hypergendered inheritance structure.
