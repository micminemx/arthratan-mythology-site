Part XXXIII — Authoritative Hypercategorical and Hyperordinal Revision 
180. Why this revision exists 
This part is authoritative over any older wording that treated the entire female ladder as “Hyperfemale/HF” or 
the entire male ladder as “Hypermale/HM”. The earlier ladder has not been deleted; it has been reclassified. 
What older editions called the Hyperfemale ladder is now the Suprafemale ladder, written SF. What older 
editions called the Hypermale ladder is now the Supramale ladder, written SM. The names Hyperfemale and 
Hypermale are now reserved for a later and categorically higher developmental region. 
The revision now distinguishes a global five-coordinate Archstage envelope from the InternalHGLDescriptor 
used to describe the constitutive logic realized at an address. These layers must never be collapsed: 
Hyperstage answers: which unbounded outer Archwomanhood/Archmanhood hyperstage is active? There is 
no greatest Hyperstage. 
Stage answers: which bounded major developmental stage inside the current Hyperstage is active? Stage is 
bounded by Omega_H within each Hyperstage. 
Substage, Substage^2, and Substage^3 supply progressively finer bounded coordinates inside the current 
Stage. In the base named-category slice, Substage^2 is the category selector (1=Supra, 2=Hyper, 3=Ultra, 
4=Apex) and Substage^3 supplies the category-local superscript coordinate n. The older 
minor-category/dimensional-order/vector machinery remains a typed InternalHGLDescriptor and can be 
canonically encoded within these bounded coordinates without becoming an additional global stage 
dimension. 
The complete current global developmental address is therefore represented schematically by 
A_F = (H, S, U, U2, U3) 
for Archwomanhood, where H=Hyperstage, S=Stage, U=Substage, U2=Substage^2, and U3=Substage^3, 
and 
A_M = (H, S, U, U2, U3) 
for Archmanhood. H ranges over an unbounded ordinal-like HGL Hyperstage scale with no maximal value. 
Each of S,U,U2,U3 is restricted to the current Hyperstage's Omega_H-bounded domain. 
InternalHGLDescriptor(category, dimensional_order, coordinate_vector, provenance) is stored separately or 
obtained by a typed decoding map; it is not a sixth stage coordinate. 
A Hyperstage jump, Stage jump, Substage jump, Substage^2 jump, and Substage^3 jump are five different 
coordinate operations. Internal HGL coordinate succession, dimensional lifting, category lifting, and limit 
closure remain constitutive mechanisms that determine which HGL state is realized at a given global 
address; they are not synonyms for the endocrine address coordinates.
