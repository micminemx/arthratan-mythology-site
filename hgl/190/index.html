189. Cardinal size versus hyperdimensional address structure of one major stage 
A useful mathematical deduction survives the SIH revision, but its interpretation changes because a higher 
minor category is not one flat ordinal block. 
Assume each active coordinate axis has cardinality aleph_omega and each adopted dimensional-order 
domain has cardinality no greater than aleph_omega. Then a Hyper/Ultra/Apex minor category can contain 
aleph_omega many canonical coordinate-addresses even though those addresses have a much richer 
multidimensional organization. A major stage containing aleph-null many such minor categories still has 
cardinality aleph_omega under ordinary infinite-cardinal multiplication.
