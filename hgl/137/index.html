235. Macroscopic Manifestation Axiom 
Domain governance alone would still be compatible with a stage whose actual macroscopic output happened 
to be indistinguishable from a non-Archstage realization in every respect. The present canon deliberately 
strengthens the system beyond that weak reading. Every valid Archstage must actually manifest at least 
some stage-governed gross-anatomical phenotype and at least some stage-governed outwardly apparent 
phenotype. This is an existence requirement, not an assertion that every Archstage trait is visible or that 
every stage has one uniquely diagnostic visible marker. 
MACROSCOPIC MANIFESTATION AXIOM (female side)​
For each n >= 1, let M^F_n be the nonempty set/class of Stage-n-governed macroscopic 
anatomical phenotype alternatives and V^F_n the nonempty set/class of Stage-n-governed 
outwardly apparent phenotype alternatives. For every valid SF^n realization:​
​
□^F_n( OR_{m in M^F_n} m )​
□^F_n( OR_{v in V^F_n} v )​
​
The male side mirrors this with M^M_n and V^M_n. 
The disjunctive form is intentional. It allows stage-valid variation. HGL need not force every Archfemale at 
SF^7 to have one identical Stage-7 visible morphology. Instead every valid SF^7 realization must express at 
least one appropriate member of the Stage-7 macroscopic class and at least one appropriate member of the 
Stage-7 outward class. A later biological law may make particular traits individually necessary, but the 
general axiom requires only nonempty stage-conditioned macro/outward manifestation. 
236. What the new axiom does not say 
It does not say every Archwomanhood/Archmanhood phenotype is visible. 
It does not say every new higher-stage novelty is visible. 
It does not say every microscopic change has a visible consequence. 
It does not say two different stages must be visually distinguishable by an unaided observer in every context. 
It does not say a higher stage must be larger, more exaggerated, more stereotypical, or more sexually 
attractive. 
It does not say the same visible trait occurs in every tissue; Γ is global governance, not uniform local output. 
It does not give permission to infer exact breast shape, genital morphology, body proportions, facial 
morphology, or any other concrete trait without a phenotype law. 
The strongest corrected statement is therefore: total macroscopic/outward silence is not a valid Archstage 
realization, but total visual diagnostic uniqueness is also not required. 
237. Global metaexpression and local physical output 
The Archwomanhood meta-phenotype remains a mathematical function or rule-regime rather than a single 
ordinary physical trait. This higher-order status is exactly why its influence is whole-body. But a function 
operating globally can return different outputs for different local biological inputs. The skin, breast tissue, 
reproductive anatomy, bone, muscle, vasculature, nervous system, and every other region can express 
different physical phenotypes while all are constrained by the same Γ^F_n. 
For region r: P_r(t) = Γ^F_n(B_r(t), H_r(t), E_r(t), D(t)).
