Part XXVIII — The IH Inheritance Foundation: PA irreducibility, stage 
recursion, and the transition to explicit phenotype logic 
This part records the IH inheritance foundation on which the later PH phenotype-possession revision is built. 
The authoritative phenotype-complete formulation appears in Part XXIX and supersedes any 
phenotype-incomplete formula below where the two differ. This part still supersedes older passages that 
used the former three-term SF2/SM2 as the complete Stage-2 identity, treated a provisional whole-state 
antithesis operator as the canonical higher recursion, relied on PA monotonicity, defined 
Archfemale/Archmale by the primitive PA route rather than type membership, or suggested nested PA as a 
required source of unbounded depth. 
The current architecture has four different engines: 
1.​
PA irreducibility creates distinct causal-developmental modes from distinct inner conditions. 
2.​
IH inheritance preserves source-sensitive properties and genealogy. 
3.​
PH phenotype possession represents irreducible possession of the complete phenotype-profile 
associated with a condition, state, or non-state source. 
4.​
ordinary Boolean logic, including De Morgan, analyzes conditions and state projections at the level 
where its connectives occur. 
No one engine may silently take over the job of another. PA is not PH; PH is not IH; IH is not ordinary 
implication; and Boolean negation outside any operator is not automatically the same as applying that 
operator to negated content.
