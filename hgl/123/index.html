245. Hidden dimensions as modal-space expansion 
The hidden-dimension thesis now receives a precise modal reading. A higher stage can reveal a phenotype 
dimension q that was not biologically expressible under the lower stage. 
NOT ◇^F_n q AND ◇^F_(n+1) q. 
If the higher stage requires q rather than merely permitting it: 
NOT ◇^F_n q AND □^F_(n+1) q. 
This is not “more than 100% Female.” It is a change in the topology and vocabulary of female-directed 
phenotype expressibility. 
246. Hidden dimensions as new relations rather than new objects 
A new dimension need not be a new organ or visibly dramatic structure. It can be a new relation among old 
structures. 
Stage n: ◇_n A AND ◇_n B, but relation R(A,B) is unavailable. 
Stage n+1: ◇_(n+1) R(A,B). 
For example, a previously impossible timing relationship between two endocrine systems, a new 
coordination rule between microanatomy and circulation, or a new repair response can count as a 
higher-stage phenotypic dimension even if all individual organs were already present. 
247. Stage stagnation theorem in metaexpression form 
Let s_F(t)=n throughout an interval I. Then the current Archwomanhood meta-phenotype is fixed: 
For all t in I: Γ^F(t) = Γ^F_n. 
But lower-order biological variables θ(t) can continue changing. Therefore: 
P(t) = F(θ(t); Γ^F_n) 
θ(t1) ≠ θ(t2) can coexist with Γ^F(t1) = Γ^F(t2). 
Therefore P(t1) need not equal P(t2). 
This is the strongest formal statement yet of the distinction between developing as a woman and advancing 
as an Archwoman. 
248. Stopping Archwomanhood phenotypes means stopping meta-rule 
advancement 
The phrase “Archwomanhood phenotypes stop developing” must now be interpreted precisely. It means that 
the subject stops acquiring successively higher stage meta-phenotypes Γ^F_(n+1), Γ^F_(n+2), and so on. It 
does not mean that every physical phenotype produced under Γ^F_n freezes. 
no Γ^F_(n+1) acquisition ≠ no new P(t).
