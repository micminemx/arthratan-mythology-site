241. Why a meta-phenotype can have visually obvious consequences 
A common category error is to assume that because Γ is meta-level, its consequences must be abstract or 
invisible. That inference is invalid. A compiler rule is abstract but determines concrete machine output; a 
building code is abstract but shapes visible buildings; a developmental rule is abstract but can shape gross 
anatomy. Likewise an Archstage meta-phenotype can be a higher-order rule while producing conspicuous 
physical anatomy. 
META DOES NOT MEAN MICROSCOPIC​
The order of a rule and the scale of its output are independent variables. A high-order rule can 
govern a low-level molecular event, a tissue event, a whole organ, the geometry of the body, an 
externally visible trait, or a relation among all of them. 
242. Ordinary womanhood versus low Archwomanhood versus high 
Archwomanhood 
The phenotype comparison is now two-dimensional. One axis is ordinary female developmental 
realization—age, puberty, maturation, history, and other female biological variables. The second axis is the 
Archwomanhood metaexpressive stage Γ^F_n. The same womanhood developmental input can therefore 
map to different physical outputs under different Archwomanhood stages, and the same Archstage can map 
changing womanhood inputs to different outputs over time. 
Ordinary womanhood (outside the Archwomanhood ladder) 
Ordinary womanhood contains ordinary female phenotypes across microscopic, physiological, macroscopic, 
and outward domains. Variation can be local or whole-body. No Archwomanhood Γ^F_n is required to 
explain the phenotype-space. 
Low Archwomanhood 
At SF^1 and SF^2, the body is already globally governed by an Archwomanhood metaexpressive regime. 
Stage-specific gross anatomical and outward expression is therefore present to some extent, while the stage 
also carries microscopic, physiological, and causal-developmental distinctions. A low stage can retain many 
familiar female phenotype categories while changing their admissibility rules, relationships, timing, or exact 
physical realization. 
Intermediate Archwomanhood 
At intermediate stages, inherited state structure and whole-source PH relations can support more elaborate 
modal constraints. Macroscopic phenotype can become increasingly relational: a body contour may depend 
on a skeletal relation; organ morphology may be permitted only together with a tissue architecture; an 
outward trait may be constrained by an endocrine or developmental condition. These are examples of 
possible rule-structure, not mandatory concrete forms. 
Very high Archwomanhood 
At very high finite orders such as SF^20, SF^50, or SF^100, the Archwomanhood meta-phenotype can 
govern an extraordinarily deep rule-space. The actual body may therefore exhibit macroscopic anatomy and
