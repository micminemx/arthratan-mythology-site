202. Aliza at SF3-SF5 
At Stage 3, 
PH(SF2), NOT PH(SM2) 
become explicit. At Stage 4, PH(SF3) and NOT PH(SM3) become explicit; and so on. The opposite 
structured nonstate continues to enter through IH(NOT SM_n), not through a constitutive PH(NOT SM_n) 
source. 
Possible qualitative changes include: 
•​
phenotypes whose causes are themselves lower-stage whole phenotypes; 
•​
regulatory systems that coordinate several earlier stage-specific tissues; 
•​
delayed phenotype expression that only becomes possible after multiple profile sources coexist; 
•​
anatomies that are difficult to predict from ordinary female biology but become straightforward once 
the source graph is known; 
•​
phenotypes with no familiar ordinary-woman analogue yet still fully female-typed in HGL. 
The phrase hidden dimensions of femaleness is most appropriate here: the high-stage phenotype need not 
resemble a caricature of familiar femininity. It can instead reveal a biological possibility that ordinary 
womanhood never had reason to instantiate. 
203. Very high Archwomanhood 
At SF50 or SF100, HGL still does not license arbitrary “alien” traits. The whole is female-typed and is 
recursively grounded in the female-side ladder. 
What changes is the depth of source structure. A Stage-100 phenotype source can be the whole phenotype 
of Stage 99, whose own definition references Stage 98, and so on. 
A high-stage trait may therefore be: 
•​
microscopic despite deep recursion; 
•​
macroscopic but not stereotypically feminine; 
•​
a new organ-level organization; 
•​
a physiological timing relation; 
•​
a tissue compatibility rule; 
•​
a developmental self-regulation pattern; 
•​
a new relation among existing structures rather than a new structure; 
•​
a property expressed only during a particular ordinary female maturity phase; 
•​
an emergent phenotype that becomes obvious in hindsight once its inheritance/PH genealogy is 
traced. 
None of these examples is forced merely by stage number.
