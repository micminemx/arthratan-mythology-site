417. Proof-status discipline after the repair 
D means forced by the current definitions/axioms and licensed proof rules. HI means a structural inference 
supported by the architecture but not forced in all models. CD requires an explicit causal law plus proved 
antecedents. CI means a plausible generative/biological relationship without a complete forcing law. ILL is an 
explanatory example. OPEN is reserved for genuinely uncanonized choices such as PH-10 strict profile 
novelty or future NOT-FD strengthening of higher categories. No compiler may silently promote HI/CI/ILL to 
D/CD. 
418. PH-10 remains explicitly open

coordinate, dimensional, category, and limit constructor separately.
