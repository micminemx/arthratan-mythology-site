HF1 = SIH_axis(SF1) AND SIH_axis(NOT SM1). 
HM1 = SIH_axis(SM1) AND SIH_axis(NOT SF1). 
HF1/HM1 remain special two-SIH category seeds. They superinherit the complete foundational axes but do

For n>=1: HF_(n+1) = SIH_axis(HF_n) AND SIH_axis(NOT HM_n) AND PH(HF_n) AND NOT 
PH(HM_n) AND UD(HF_n). 
For n>=1: HM_(n+1) = SIH_axis(HM_n) AND SIH_axis(NOT HF_n) AND PH(HM_n) AND NOT

HF2+ therefore has exactly five top-level conjuncts. SIH supplies complete axis provenance, PH supplies 
positive same-side whole-profile possession, NOT-PH excludes complete opposite whole-profile possession, 
and UD makes the own-side predecessor a significant whole-form Unflaw contribution. These are four 
different semantic operator families despite their shared predecessor indices. 
360. Hyper profile-exclusion noncollapse theorem 
SIH_axis(NOT HM_n) != NOT PH(HM_n) 
PH(HF_n) != SIH_axis(HF_n) 
UD(HF_n) != PH(HF_n) 
From HF_(n+1) one may deduce all five conjuncts by conjunction elimination, but no one conjunct is licensed 
merely by another. In particular, SIH(HF_n) does not automatically create PH(HF_n), and SIH(NOT HM_n) 
does not automatically create NOT PH(HM_n). Their explicit appearance in the formula is therefore logically 
substantive rather than decorative duplication. 
361. Hyper dimensional quality consequence and its limit 
Because HF2+ explicitly contains UD(HF_n), it satisfies the antecedent needed by the Dimensional Unflaw 
Quality Bridge when the corresponding DimLift relation is present. Hence the quality increase can be a 
deduction relative to that bridge. However, the formula still does not contain NOT FD(HF_n). Therefore no 
theorem may add local nonflawhood of HF_n merely from the five-conjunct Hyper recurrence. 
DimLift(HF_n,HF_(n+1)) AND UD_target(HF_n) => SignificantIncrease(Q(HF_n),Q(HF_(n+1))). 
HF_(n+1) does NOT by definition entail NOT FD(HF_n). 
At transfinite quality magnitudes, the established significant-quality semantics then force at least a 
successor-cardinal magnitude increase. This is a whole-form quality theorem, not a theorem that every 
visible anatomical metric grows.
