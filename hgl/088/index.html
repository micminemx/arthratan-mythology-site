Inherited own-side preservation: IHSFn or IHSMn carries forward the full own-side preceding state with its

Inherited opposite-side non-state productivity: IH¬SMn or IH¬SFn carries forward the structured logical

Whole own-side phenotype preservation: PHSFn or PHSMn makes the complete phenotype-profile of the

opposite predecessor phenotype-profile. This is not positive possession of a nonstate-profile and does not 
erase the separately inherited structured nonstate carried by IH(NOT opposite). 
The combination means the ladder does not merely oscillate between fixed Boolean formulas. Each side 
recursively preserves its own logical/provenance history, absorbs structured information about a new 
opposite-side nonstate, positively possesses its own immediately preceding whole phenotype-profile, 
explicitly excludes the opposite predecessor whole-profile, and quality-types only the own-side predecessor 
through UD plus NOT-FD.
