Part XXIV — Remaining open questions after the IH revision 
The following are genuine open variables. Earlier questions about the canonical whole-state antithesis 
operator, SF2 conflict, and Stage-1 female typing are closed by the present revision and are no longer open. 
75. Which logically equivalent source formulas count as the same IH source? 
The conservative assumption is that classically equivalent descriptions of one source state can be 
normalized without changing source identity, while derivational provenance is retained separately. A future 
intensional refinement could distinguish syntactic histories even when Boolean content is equivalent. 
76. Exactly which properties are “inheritable properties”? 
Core IH projection says all source-state content relevant to HGL is retained. A fuller theory could partition 
properties into constitutive, optional, transient, or noninheritable classes. Until then, IH(S) should be read 
as inheritance of the source state’s HGL-defining properties, not literally every accidental fact true of the 
individual who once occupied S. 
77. Can IH ever distribute under a special law? 
Core HGL says no automatic distribution. A future rule could state that particular outer conjunctions are 
componentwise inheritable, but such a rule must preserve PA irreducibility and source provenance. 
78. Can a stage be lost or regress? 
The present ladder defines upward construction and permits stagnation. Regression requires a separate 
temporal law. 
79. Can stages be skipped? 
Not specified. The canonical recursive definition gives an order, but a causal transition theory would need to 
say whether an individual must physically pass through every preceding stage.
