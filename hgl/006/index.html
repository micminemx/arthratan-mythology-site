automatically imply stage advancement.

Hypergendered Logic is a recursive biological metaexpression logic in which PA preserves 
irreducible developmental causation, IH preserves inherited logical/state provenance, PH

governs the modal rules of physical phenotype expression. Higher 
Archwomanhood/Archmanhood stages recursively carry forward prior structure while changing or 
enriching the biological expression regime, without turning stage progression into a percentage 
increase of Female or Male.
