51. Comparative phenotype table in words 
The most careful comparative descriptions are: 
Ordinary woman: female biology without a specified Archwomanhood inheritance ladder. 
Low-stage Archfemale: female biology with a small number of explicitly defined irreducible and inherited HGL 
structures. 
High-stage Archfemale: female biology with deep inheritance provenance whose phenotype may require 
high-order source relations to explain. 
Ordinary man: male biology without a specified Archmanhood inheritance ladder. 
Low-stage Archmale: male biology with early Supramale irreducible and inherited structures. 
High-stage Archmale: male biology with deep male-side/non-Suprafemale inheritance genealogy and 
potentially qualitatively novel male-compatible phenotypes. 
The hierarchy therefore predicts greater formal developmental depth, not a universal monotone ordering of 
visible anatomy, hormone quantity, stereotypical appearance, health, strength, fertility, attractiveness, or 
maturity.
