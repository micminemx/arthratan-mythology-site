272. A detailed plateau example for an Archfemale 
Aliza reaches SF^6 before completing puberty. From that point onward, suppose she permanently stops 
Archwomanhood progression. Her stage rule remains Γ^F_6. 
For every later t: s_F(t)=6 and Γ^F(t)=Γ^F_6. 
At age 13, her lower-level biological parameter vector is θ_13. At 15 it is θ_15; at 18 it is θ_18. These states 
differ because ordinary female development continues. 
P_13 = F(θ_13; Γ^F_6) 
P_15 = F(θ_15; Γ^F_6) 
P_18 = F(θ_18; Γ^F_6). 
P_13, P_15, and P_18 may all differ even though Γ^F_6 never changes. New breasts/tissue maturation, 
skeletal maturation, endocrine patterns, microscopic tissue changes, or other fictional female phenotypes 
can emerge gradually if the ordinary female developmental laws and Γ^F_6 permit them. None of this is 
SF^7 unless the stage itself advances. 
273. A detailed plateau example for an Archmale 
The exact mirror holds for an Archmale stalled at SM^6. Γ^M_6 remains global and static while ordinary male 
biological parameters continue changing. New male phenotypes can appear through puberty, maturation, 
aging, injury response, or other permitted biology without SM^7 being reached. 
274. Why this is stronger than a “whole-body trait” theory 
If an Archwomanhood phenotype were merely a whole-body trait, the theory would have to explain how one 
trait could literally be identical in skin, bone, liver, brain, blood, and reproductive anatomy. The 
meta-phenotype interpretation avoids that category error. What is global is the rule-regime, not the local trait 
identity. 
It is therefore more powerful to say that Stage 20 globally changes the laws of biological phenotype 
expressibility than to say that Stage 20 paints the same special trait onto every tissue. 
275. Why this makes modal logic an extension rather than an analogy 
Modal logic is not being added merely because words such as “possible” and “necessary” sound useful. Γ 
literally defines a space of admissible realizations. Quantifying over that space is modal semantics. 
necessity = truth in all Γ-valid phenotype realizations. 
possibility = truth in at least one Γ-valid phenotype realization. 
impossibility = truth in none of the Γ-valid phenotype realizations. 
The modal extension is therefore a direct formalization of Biological Metaexpressability. 
276. Developmental modality generated around PA 
A parallel but distinct modal layer can be used to reason about realizations of a PA developmental mode. Let 
D_φ be the set of developmental realizations compatible with the irreducible PA source φ. 
□^D_φ q := q holds in every admissible developmental realization of PA(φ). 
◇^D_φ q := q holds in at least one admissible developmental realization of PA(φ). 
These modal operators are not identical to PA itself. PA(φ) establishes the developmental causal mode due 
to φ. □^D_φ and ◇^D_φ quantify over how that mode may be realized.
