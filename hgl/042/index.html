59. Possible Archmale phenotype families 
The male side admits the same types of novelty: joint-cause microanatomy, separately routed Stage-2 
structures, provenance-sensitive differentiation, high-order tissue organization, stage-specific endocrine 
topology, repair systems, vascular organization, neural regulation, metabolic coordination, or developmental 
timing. The content is male-directed because the stage is male-typed and built through the SM recursion. 
A higher Archmale need not be taller, more muscular, more fertile, more aggressive, deeper-voiced, or more 
externally masculine than every lower-stage Archmale. Those would require explicit membership in a 
relevant PH profile or a causal phenotype law.
