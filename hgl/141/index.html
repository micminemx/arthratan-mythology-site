247. Stage advancement can be visually transformative without being visually 
monotone 
When SF^n advances to SF^(n+1), Γ changes. That can change visible anatomy, but there is no theorem 
that each higher stage must be visually “more” in a single direction. A stage may increase one dimension, 
reduce another, introduce a new relation, stabilize a previously variable trait, permit a new combination, or 
replace one morphology with another. What increases monotonically is recursive stage order under the 
ladder rules, not size, visibility, attractiveness, stereotypical femininity/masculinity, or any one anatomical 
measurement. 
248. Logical deductions licensed by the macroscopic correction 
D1. Valid SF^n -> at least one Γ^F_n-governed macroscopic anatomical phenotype is actual. 
D2. Valid SF^n -> at least one Γ^F_n-governed outwardly apparent phenotype is actual. 
D3. Valid SM^n -> at least one Γ^M_n-governed macroscopic anatomical phenotype is actual. 
D4. Valid SM^n -> at least one Γ^M_n-governed outwardly apparent phenotype is actual. 
D5. No valid Archstage realization is wholly macro/outward silent. 
D6. D1-D5 do not identify which exact visible trait is necessary because the general axiom is disjunctive. 
D7. Global Γ-governance does not entail identical phenotype in different regions. 
D8. Static Γ does not entail static macroscopic/outward phenotype. 
D9. New visible phenotype does not entail stage advancement. 
D10. If □^F_n p for macroscopic p, valid SF^n entails p; symmetric male result. 
D11. If NOT ◇^F_n p, no valid SF^n realization can express p; symmetric male result. 
D12. Actual visible p in valid SF^n entails ◇^F_n p, but ◇^F_n p does not entail actual p. 
D13. Different Archstage does not logically entail a unique different outward appearance unless injective 
phenotype mapping is separately axiomatized. 
D14. Same outward appearance does not entail same stage because different Γ functions may share current 
outputs while differing counterfactually. 
249. Logical inferences that are strong but not theorems 
HI1. Higher stages will often have more structurally constrained macroscopic phenotype combinations 
because they carry deeper inherited and modal rule structure. 
HI2. Very high stages are likely to be retrospectively identifiable through combinations of visible and invisible 
evidence even when no single trait uniquely identifies the stage. 
HI3. As Γ becomes more structurally rich, a greater fraction of phenotype significance may lie in relationships 
among traits rather than isolated trait presence. 
HI4. High-stage outward anatomy may look surprising while remaining female-typed or male-typed because 
the surprise can arise from previously unavailable rule-combinations rather than category departure. 
HI5. Visual differences between adjacent high stages may sometimes be smaller than differences in latent 
possibility-space, even though every stage retains some stage-governed macro/outward expression. 
250. Causal deductions with explicit macroscopic mapping laws 
A causal deduction requires an explicit fictional biological bridge. The following are schemas, not currently 
universal HGL facts. 
If Law L1 says Γ^F_7 AND PubertyPhase_3 -> MacroTrait_A, and both antecedents obtain, then 
MacroTrait_A follows. [CD] 
If Law L2 says Γ^F_12 AND developmental variable d -> outward phenotype v, then v follows when d is 
satisfied. [CD] 
If Law L3 says Γ^M_9 AND maturity state m -> gross anatomical relation r, then r follows. [CD]
