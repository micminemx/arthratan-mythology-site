243. Archmanhood in exact modal-anatomical parallel 
Everything in the preceding sections has a male-side mirror. Γ^M_n globally governs male-typed phenotype 
expression, including gross anatomy and outward appearance. Every valid SM^n realization therefore has at 
least some stage-governed macroscopic anatomical expression and at least some outwardly apparent 
expression. Higher Archmanhood changes the rules of male phenotype-space rather than merely increasing 
a scalar “masculinity amount.” 
Possible macro/outward domains include gross male reproductive anatomy, external genital morphology, 
chest/pectoral morphology, skeletal and facial geometry, body contour, skin/hair appearance, and 
relationships among these and internal physiological systems. These examples mark domains of possible 
stage action; they do not specify exact shapes, sizes, attractiveness, or other unstated concrete outcomes. 
244. Stage stagnation with continuing visible womanhood/manhood development 
The new macroscopic rule strengthens the earlier stagnation distinction. If an Archfemale remains 
permanently at SF^6, Γ^F_6 remains the current Archwomanhood rule-function. This freezes the 
Archwomanhood stage, not the woman. Her puberty, maturation, aging, tissue growth, and other ordinary 
female developmental variables can continue changing. Because those changing variables are still 
processed under Γ^F_6, the visible body and gross anatomy can continue changing while the 
Archwomanhood stage remains exactly the same. 
Γ^F_6(t1) = Γ^F_6(t2) does NOT imply P_macro/outward(t1) = P_macro/outward(t2). 
Likewise for Archmales: Γ^M_n constant does NOT imply a static visible male phenotype. 
This is the precise formal meaning of “she continues developing as a woman, just not as an Archwoman.” 
The Archwomanhood hyperparameter is fixed; the ordinary female parameters continue changing; the output 
can therefore continue changing. 
245. Breasts, genital anatomy, and other gross anatomy as examples of ordinary 
phenotype outputs under Γ 
Gross sex-related anatomy makes the distinction especially easy to understand. Breast anatomy is 
phenotype. Gross reproductive anatomy is phenotype. Vaginal anatomy is macroscopic phenotype even 
though most of it is internal. External genital anatomy is both macroscopic and outwardly apparent. These 
and other female anatomical characteristics can therefore be constrained by Γ^F_n in exactly the same 
formal sense as any other phenotype variable. The male side is symmetric for male-typed gross reproductive 
and outward anatomy. 
However, HGL does not infer a particular size, shape, proportion, developmental timing, or stage ranking for 
any such structure unless a separate phenotype law says so. The new rule establishes domain inclusion and 
some nonempty macro/outward manifestation, not a detailed anatomical catalogue. 
246. Static-stage emergence can be visibly macroscopic 
Earlier explanations emphasized that a new phenotype can emerge during a stage plateau. The present 
correction makes explicit that such emergence can be gross anatomical and outwardly visible. Suppose 
SF^5 is static throughout puberty. A maturity variable d(t) can change while Γ^F_5 remains constant. If the 
fictional causal biology contains a rule mapping Γ^F_5 together with d(t) to a new breast, skeletal, facial, 
skin, body-contour, or reproductive phenotype, the new visible phenotype can appear without SF^6 ever 
being reached.
