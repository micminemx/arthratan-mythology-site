259. Logician explanation: nonempty visible projection without injectivity 
Let W^F_n be the Stage-n phenotype realization space and π_vis the projection from a complete phenotype 
world to its outwardly apparent phenotype coordinates. The corrected canon requires that the 
stage-governed visible projection be nonempty in every valid world, but does not require π_vis to be injective 
across stages. Thus two stages can share the same visible projection even while differing in hidden 
coordinates or modal accessibility relations. 
For all w in W^F_n: StageGoverned(π_vis(w)) is nonempty. 
But n != m does NOT entail π_vis(W^F_n) ∩ π_vis(W^F_m) = empty. 
This formalizes the exact balance intended by the new canon: some outward manifestation is mandatory; 
unique visual stage-identifiability is not. 
260. Scientist explanation: measurement program 
A fictional HGL biologist should therefore measure both the outward/macroscopic phenotype and the latent

the same time, visible morphology alone is not a complete assay of Γ because two rule-systems can produce 
overlapping current outputs while differing in what they permit under puberty, injury, aging, hormonal 
conditions, environmental change, or other counterfactual inputs.

261. Twenty anticipated questions 
Q1. If Γ is global, must every trait occur everywhere? No. Global means the rule-function governs every 
relevant region; local inputs can yield different local outputs. 
Q2. Can an Archwomanhood stage be completely invisible? No under the Macroscopic Manifestation Axiom. 
Some macro/outward stage-governed expression is required, though much additional novelty can be 
invisible. 
Q3. Does every stage have a unique visible marker? No. Different stages can overlap visibly and differ in 
latent/modal structure. 
Q4. Are breasts phenotypes? Yes. They are macroscopic anatomical phenotypes and can also be outwardly 
apparent. 
Q5. Is vaginal anatomy phenotype? Yes. It is macroscopic anatomical phenotype even though much of it is 
internal rather than outwardly visible. 
Q6. Are genital phenotypes automatically the only important Archstage outputs? No. They are examples 
within a much broader phenotype domain. 
Q7. Does higher Archwomanhood mean larger breasts or a more exaggerated body? No such monotone 
anatomical rule follows from stage order. 
Q8. Can a high stage visibly look surprising? Yes. Meta-level rules can generate conspicuous gross 
anatomy. 
Q9. Can much of a high stage still be microscopic? Yes. Mandatory nonempty macro/outward manifestation 
is compatible with extensive microscopic novelty.
