This recursion contains two preservation/generation pairs. 
•​
IH(SF^n) or IH(SM^n) preserves own-side history. 
•​
IH(¬opposite^n) makes the opposite whole’s logical non-state inherited structure. 
•​
PH(own^n) makes the complete prior own-side whole an explicit phenotype source. 
•​
PH(¬opposite^n) makes the complete non-opposite whole an explicit positive phenotype source. 
That is the mature meaning of antithetically productive zig-zag logic. 
The theory still rejects PA(PA(...)) operator nesting in core HGL. It does not reject nested logical functions 
inside a single PA argument: PA may contain arbitrarily structured Boolean conditions, and that entire inner 
structure is treated as one irreducible developmental causal basis. Recursive Archstage depth is carried by 
IH/PH whole-state sources and the stage metaexpression architecture rather than by stacking PA operators. 
The hierarchy remains female/male typed without becoming scalar: 
FemaleTypeSFn,  MaleTypeSMn. 
Higher Archwomanhood therefore means neither 200%, 300%, nor 10,000% Female. Higher Archmanhood 
likewise does not mean a numeric overfilling of Male. What grows is structured biological dimensionality. 
The PH and metaexpression revisions sharpen the hidden-dimension thesis. HGL explicitly says that higher 
stages possess whole-state PH profiles while also operating under distinct global 
Archwomanhood/Archmanhood meta-phenotype functions Γ. A higher-stage difference can therefore be a 
difference in the rules of expression themselves—even before one names a particular visible trait. 
High-stage physical phenotypes can be microscopic, macroscopic, anatomical, physiological, regulatory, 
temporal, relational, or locally expressed while still being globally governed by Γ. They need not resemble a 
stereotyped exaggeration of familiar womanhood/manhood.

Finally, Archstage progression remains distinct from ordinary woman/man development. An Archfemale can 
become permanently stuck at SF5 so that Γ^F_5 remains the current global Archwomanhood 
meta-phenotype indefinitely. She can nevertheless continue puberty, maturation, aging, repair, ordinary 
female phenotype emergence, and new interactions among already-present structures. The static Γ^F_5 
acts as the unchanged hyperparameter while changing womanhood biology supplies changing parameters. 
New outputs do not constitute SF6 unless the stage rule itself advances. 
The strongest lay summary is: 
Archwomanhood is not a volume knob that turns Female above 100%. It is a recursive ladder in 
which each stage keeps female-side history, carries structured non-male-side information, 
possesses whole PH profiles of increasingly rich sources, and globally establishes a 
stage-specific meta-phenotype Γ that functions like a biological hyperparameter over actual 
physical phenotypes. If the ladder stops, Γ stops changing; the woman does not stop developing 
as a woman because the ordinary biological parameters passed through Γ can keep changing.
