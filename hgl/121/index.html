235. Model-theoretic semantics 
Let W^F_n(B,H,E) be the nonempty set of phenotype realizations compatible with Γ^F_n for biological state 
B, developmental history H, and environment E. Let P_actual be the current realized phenotype. 
P_actual ∈ W^F_n(B,H,E). 
□^F_n φ iff for every P in W^F_n(B,H,E), P satisfies φ. 
◇^F_n φ iff for some P in W^F_n(B,H,E), P satisfies φ. 
This semantics immediately explains why the Archstage meta-phenotype is global: Γ generates or constrains 
the admissible whole-body realization-space, while the elements of each realization can contain arbitrarily 
local traits. 
236. Minimal modal laws adopted by HGL 
The stage-relative phenotype modality is treated as at least a normal, actual-world-valid biological modality. 
The following rules are safe under the model above. 
Duality: ◇_n p ↔ NOT □_n NOT p. 
K: □_n(p -> q) -> (□_n p -> □_n q). 
T/factivity: □_n p -> p, for a valid current Stage-n realization. 
Nonemptiness: □_n p -> ◇_n p. 
HGL does not automatically adopt S4, S5, symmetry, Euclideanness, or transitivity across biological 
phenotype worlds. Those frame properties would require biological justification. 
237. Actuality implies biological possibility 
If a subject is a valid Stage-n Archfemale and actually expresses p, p must be among the realizations 
licensed by Γ^F_n. 
p_actual -> ◇^F_n p. 
This is a strict deduction only relative to the assumption that the observed subject is correctly classified and 
biologically valid under the stated Γ. If the observation appears to violate the modality, then at least one of the 
stage classification, phenotype observation, or Γ rule-set is wrong/incomplete. 
238. Biological impossibility excludes actuality 
NOT ◇^F_n p -> NOT p_actual. 
This is stronger than saying p is unusual. It says p is outside the stage-valid expression-space. Again, the 
impossibility is stage-relative biological impossibility, not metaphysical impossibility. 
239. Necessity versus mere possession 
PH(p-source) and □_n p answer different questions. PH can establish possession of a source-defined 
phenotype-profile. □_n p says every admissible current-stage realization has p. A trait can be actually 
possessed without being necessary across every admissible realization.
