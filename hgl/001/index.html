Preface: what this document is 
Hypergendered Logic (HGL) is the name given here to an invented formal field for reasoning about a 
recursively and hyperordinally defined fictional biology of Female, Male, Unfemale, Unmale, 
Suprafemale/Supramale, Hyperfemale/Hypermale, Ultrafemale/Ultramale, Apexfemale/Apexmale, further 
minor hypercategories, and the major-stage ladders of Archwomanhood and Archmanhood. The purpose of 
this document is not merely to list definitions. Its purpose is to make the underlying logic inspectable: to show 
where negation is located, which transformations are ordinary classical logic, which transformations are 
special HGL operations, which claims are strict deductions, which are logical inferences, which are causal 
deductions, which are causal inferences, and which phenotype examples are illustrative rather than forced. 
This is a fictional formal system, not a medical theory of real human sex development. In HGL, XX and XY 
are treated as formal chromosome-condition predicates because that is how the system has been defined. 
Real biological sex development is more complex than a two-predicate model. Accordingly, every biological 
example in this treatise is either (a) internal to the fictional HGL system, or (b) explicitly marked as an 
illustrative hypothetical phenotype rather than a real-world biological claim.
