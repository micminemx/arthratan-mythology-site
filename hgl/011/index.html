8. No nested PA, no self-referential same-stage PH 
The phrase “no nested PA” is a type restriction on PA as an operator, not a restriction on logical depth inside 
the PA argument. HGL permits nested propositional/logical functions inside a single PA whenever the inner 
expression is a well-formed developmental condition. 
Allowed: PA(NOT((x AND NOT y) OR (NOT x AND y))). 
Noncanonical in core HGL: PA(PA(x)). 
The allowed form is especially important under Biological Metaexpressability. A logically complex inner 
condition can specify a correspondingly complex rule/constraint basis for what anatomy and physiology are 
developmentally expressible, while PA irreducibility prevents that basis from being automatically 
decomposed into separate PA modes.
