80. Are all stage transitions instantaneous in stage identity? 
Not specified. A transition mechanism could be discrete even if phenotype realization is gradual, or could 
contain intermediate transition states. 
81. What exact phenotype map applies? 
The logic intentionally leaves underdetermined. This is the largest remaining biological-content question. 
Φ
82. Are all high-stage defining effects retained phenotypically forever? 
Stage ancestry is logically retained. Phenotypic manifestation of every downstream trait need not be 
permanent unless a causal retention law says so. 
83. Can different IH genealogies converge on the same phenotype? 
Yes as a logical possibility. Whether the world permits it depends on .

84. Can the same IH genealogy produce different phenotype under different 
environments? 
Yes unless is stipulated to make phenotype completely stage-determined. 
Φ
85. Is there now a transfinite/hyperordinal extension? 
Yes. This edition explicitly adopts a hyperordinal extension. The old finite successor recursion remains the 
local finite sector of Suprafemale/Supramale development; Hyperomega Ω_H is a stipulated HGL boundary 
with aleph-omega-many ordinal substage positions below/within the relevant local progression, and 
minor-category index ω marks transition into the next major stage. The exact causal mechanism by which a 
biological individual completes such limit transitions remains a separate causal-developmental law and must 
not be fabricated from ordinal notation alone.
