223. Canonical correction: phenotype versus meta-phenotype 
Phenotype, in the ordinary HGL sense, means a physically apparent or physically instantiated biological 
characteristic/configuration. It can be macroscopic or microscopic, local or systemic, temporary or persistent. 
Archwomanhood phenotype and Archmanhood phenotype are higher-order uses of the word phenotype. 
They are meta-phenotypes: stage-specific global rules and constraints over physical phenotype expression. 
To prevent type confusion, this treatise writes them as Γ^F_n and Γ^M_n. 
Γ^F_n := the global Stage-n Archwomanhood meta-phenotype function. 
Γ^M_n := the global Stage-n Archmanhood meta-phenotype function. 
P := an actual physically expressed phenotype state. 
The difference is the same kind of difference that exists between a parameter and a hyperparameter. P 
contains the biological outputs. Γ configures the rules that determine which outputs are available, required, 
excluded, or transformed. 
224. Why the hyperparameter analogy is structurally accurate 
Suppose a phenotype-generating system has ordinary biological variables θ: hormone concentrations, 
developmental timing, tissue state, age, local signaling, environmental inputs, inherited anatomy, and so on. 
These variables can change while the Archstage remains constant. 
P(t) = F(θ(t); Γ^F_n). 
The semicolon is conceptually important. θ contains changing lower-order biological parameters. Γ^F_n 
supplies higher-order stage rules that constrain or transform how those parameters can become phenotype. 
If Γ is fixed while θ changes, P can still change. 
ΔΓ^F_n = 0 does not imply ΔP = 0. 
This is the mathematical core of the sentence: a woman can stop developing upward as an Archwoman 
while continuing to develop as a woman. 
225. Globality means global governance, not phenotype uniformity 
Every actual physical phenotype throughout an Archfemale body is expressed under the current Γ^F_n. 
Every actual physical phenotype throughout an Archmale body is expressed under Γ^M_n. This is why the 
Archstage meta-phenotype is whole-body. 
For every relevant body region r: P_r(t) ∈ Expr^F_n(r,t). 
Yet P_skin can differ from P_bone, P_brain, P_ovary, P_muscle, P_blood, and P_cell. The global 
rule-function does not demand that every region instantiate the same trait. It demands that every region’s 
physical expression be stage-valid. 
global Γ ≠ identical P in every tissue. 
Layman analogy: the same national legal system governs a hospital, a school, a farm, and a court without 
requiring those places to perform the same activity. Γ is global in jurisdiction; phenotype is local in realization.
