254. The anatomy/visibility matrix 
To prevent future confusion, HGL should classify physical phenotype along at least two independent axes: 
scale and visibility. Macroscopic does not automatically mean outwardly visible, and microscopic does not 
mean non-phenotypic. 
Macroscopic + outward: external body contour, visible skin/hair, externally visible morphology, externally 
visible portions of gross anatomy. 
Macroscopic + internal: internal gross reproductive anatomy, internal organ morphology, organ relationships, 
gross vascular or skeletal relations not externally visible. 
Microscopic + externally sampled/observable: histology or cellular traits observable with instruments after 
sampling; physical phenotype but not unaided outward appearance. 
Microscopic + internal: tissue/cellular/microanatomical states that may contribute to higher-level phenotype 
without being directly visible.
