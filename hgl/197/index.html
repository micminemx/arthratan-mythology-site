206. Apexfemale phenotype nature 
Apexfemale begins only after the complete Ultrafemale dimensional domain is saturated and AF1 is 
constructed by SIH_dim; it is not an identity with an Ultra endpoint. 
AF1 = SIH_dim(UF-domain) AND SIH_dim(NOT UM-domain). 
Apexfemale can therefore contain the inherited categorical history Female -> Suprafemale -> Hyperfemale -> 
Ultrafemale -> Apexfemale while introducing an Apexfemale-specific rule regime. AF1 superinherits the 
complete Ultrafemale dimensional domain and the corresponding non-Ultramale dimensional domain. 
At a visible level, one possible Apexfemale phenotype may involve gross anatomical configurations 
unavailable throughout lower Ultrafemale dimensional domains; another may retain superficially similar 
external anatomy while differing radically in provenance, whole-quality organization, and the modal 
counterfactuals governing how that anatomy can develop, repair, mature, or coexist with other traits. 
This distinction is important: hypercategorical novelty can be visually dramatic, but it need not be reducible to 
visual distance.

208. The possible phenotype nature of Major Stage 2 
Major Stage 2 is more radical than merely moving from one named minor category to another, because the 
major-stage envelope itself changes. 
One possible interpretation is that Major Stage 1 determines rules over ordinary female phenotype, while 
Major Stage 2 begins to determine higher-order constraints over families of minor-stage metaexpression 
rules themselves. In that model, Major Stage 2 can reorganize not only which phenotype is possible, but 
which kinds of modal transformations between minor categories are available. 
Macroscopic consequences may therefore be extremely different from Major Stage 1. Yet the exact form 
remains causally underdetermined. A Major Stage 2 Archfemale could manifest visibly unprecedented 
female anatomy, or could retain some familiar external traits while possessing radically different 
developmental counterfactuals and whole-body integration rules.
