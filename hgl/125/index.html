253. Intermediate Archwomanhood 
At intermediate stages, the most characteristic difference may be rule-composition rather than a longer list of 
independent visible traits. Γ^F_n can contain conditions whose inputs include phenotypes generated under 
earlier rules, inherited structures, maturity state, and current physiology. 
Illustrative possibilities include: 
a macroanatomical trait whose allowed geometry depends on a microscopic Stage-(n-1) tissue phenotype; 
an endocrine phenotype whose timing is constrained by an inherited non-SM state; 
a repair phenotype that is impossible at Stage n-1 but permitted at Stage n; 
a stage-wide compatibility rule preventing two otherwise possible local phenotypes from co-occurring; 
a conditional phenotype that becomes necessary only when pregnancy, puberty, injury, or another biological 
condition is present. 
254. Very high Archwomanhood 
A very high Γ^F_n can be extraordinarily rich even when the external body remains visually simple. The 
complexity can reside in the rule-space itself: which phenotype combinations are legal, which latent 
developmental paths can activate, which interactions are required, which microstructures can coexist, and 
how previously acquired dimensions can coordinate. 
A Stage-100 Archfemale could therefore differ from a Stage-10 Archfemale by hundreds of layers of global 
metaexpressive constraints while only a fraction of those differences are externally visible at one moment. 
Possible high-stage phenotype nature: deeply context-sensitive rather than constantly expressed. 
Possible high-stage phenotype nature: microscopic or physiological rather than visually obvious. 
Possible high-stage phenotype nature: relational—defined by how multiple tissues interact rather than by one 
tissue alone. 
Possible high-stage phenotype nature: temporal—defined by allowable developmental transitions over time. 
Possible high-stage phenotype nature: counterfactual—what the body would be able or unable to express 
under conditions not currently present. 
255. Archmanhood mirror 
Every metaexpression rule above has an Archmanhood mirror. Γ^M_n is globally operative throughout the 
Archmale body while actual male phenotypes can remain local or systemic. A Stage-n Archmale can stop 
advancing as an Archman while continuing to develop as a man under a fixed Γ^M_n. 
s_M(t)=n constant -> Γ^M(t)=Γ^M_n constant, but P_male(t) may continue changing. 
As on the female side, high Archmanhood need not mean stereotypical visual exaggeration. It can mean 
deeper rules of male-directed anatomical, physiological, microscopic, regulatory, and temporal expressibility. 
256. Logical deductions introduced by the modal extension 
The following are strict deductions once the stage-relative modal semantics and validity assumptions are 
adopted. 
D1. If p is actually expressed in a valid Stage-n realization, then ◇_n p. 
D2. If □_n p, then p in the valid current realization. 
D3. If □_n p and W_n is nonempty, then ◇_n p. 
D4. If NOT ◇_n p, then NOT p in every valid current-stage realization. 
D5. ◇_n p is equivalent to NOT □_n NOT p under the adopted dual semantics. 
D6. □_n(p -> q) and □_n p entail □_n q. 
D7. If □_n(p -> q) and p is actual, then q is actual, because □_n(p -> q) gives p -> q at the actual valid world.
