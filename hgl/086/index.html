137. The revised Stage-1 meanings 
SF1 = PA(x AND NOT y) 
SM1 = PA(NOT x AND y) 
Corrected Stage 1 is PA-only on both sides. Each state contains one irreducibly compound 
developmental-causal basis. There are no constitutive IH, PH, UD, or FD terms at Stage 1. 
This does not remove IH or PH from HGL. It relocates their canonical entry point: Stage 2 inherits the 
Stage-1 whole and the opposite Stage-1 structured non-state, positively possesses the same-side Stage-1 
whole-profile, explicitly excludes the complete opposite Stage-1 profile, makes only the same-side 
predecessor UD, adds standalone PA routes, and denies FD of that same-side predecessor. 
Any earlier sentence claiming that SF1/SM1 themselves inherit x/y conditions or possess two PH profiles is 
superseded.
