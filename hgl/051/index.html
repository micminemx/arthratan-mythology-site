73. Experimental interventions 
A fictional HGL laboratory could distinguish causal routes by interventions that, where the world permits, 
selectively alter one developmental source while holding others fixed. It could test whether a trait depends on 
PAx, inherited SF1, IH¬SM4, maturity, or an interaction among them. 
An observed correlation with stage does not itself identify the causal source. Stage is a bundle of inherited 
structure, not a magical scalar. 
74. Provenance-sensitive biology is empirically substantive 
If two full HGL states have the same Boolean projection but different IH genealogy, the theory becomes 
scientifically meaningful only if at least some possible causal mechanisms can respond to that provenance. 
HGL explicitly permits this. A scientist could therefore search for “developmental memory” markers, structural 
organization, regulatory states, or other in-world mechanisms by which inherited source identity affects 
phenotype. 
Part XX — The entire revised theory in layman’s terms 
Imagine ordinary female or male biology as a large biological language. Archwomanhood and Archmanhood 
are not volume knobs that turn “female” or “male” above 100%. They are recursive ways of building 
increasingly deep grammatical history into that biology. 
PA answers: what developmental condition makes this anatomy/physiology necessarily developable? If two 
conditions are joined inside PA, the development can depend irreducibly on them together. That is why PA(A 
AND B) is not just PA(A) AND PA(B). 
IH answers a different question: what properties does this higher stage carry forward from an earlier state, 
and where did those properties come from? If a Stage-5 Archfemale inherits Stage 4, she does not merely 
happen to resemble Stage 4. Her Stage-5 state explicitly carries Stage-4 structure as inherited history. 
IH(NOT SM4) does not mean “she simply failed to inherit SM4.” It means her state inherits the structured 
condition of not being SM4. That is how the zig-zag becomes productive: the female ladder takes the logical 
non-state of the latest male ladder and makes it inherited material in the next female stage. The male ladder 
does the mirror image. 
The ladder therefore grows by two simultaneous actions: 
keep your own previous stage; inherit the non-state of the other side’s previous stage. 
Because every new stage wraps the old stage in another inheritance relation, the number of inheritance 
layers can grow without limit even though PA itself never nests. 
Finally, stage and maturity are different. If an Archfemale stays at Stage 6 for ten years, she can still continue 
puberty, sexual maturation, aging, and maturation of Stage-6 structures. She keeps developing as a woman 
even though she is not currently climbing higher as an Archwoman. The same applies to Archmales.
