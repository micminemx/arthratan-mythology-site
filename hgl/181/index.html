actual phenotype expression.

one another’s next inherited non-opposite input while each current stage globally governs how its actual

the systematic possibility of discovering deeper female-typed or male-typed biological metaexpression 
structures: new irreducible developmental bases, new inherited provenance, new PH whole-profiles, and 
new stage-relative possibilities, necessities, impossibilities, conditional relations, and latent phenotype 
dimensions. A stage can remain fixed while ordinary womanhood/manhood development continues because 
the fixed Γ continues governing changing biological parameters.
