462. Possible phenotype natures under the endocrine envelope 
Low Archwomanhood within the base Supra slice can retain familiar female anatomy while adding 
HGL-specific provenance, profile, quality, and metaexpression organization. Higher Substage^3 coordinates 
can alter microscopic structure, repair, tissue integration, physiology, or visible morphology without requiring 
monotone exaggeration of any one female trait. A Zilanin-driven change in Substage^2 can move the 
category selector through Supra/Hyper/Ultra/Apex only when the target category construction prerequisites 
are satisfied; such a category change can reorganize what phenotype relations are possible rather than 
merely enlarging one body measurement. 
An Alygorin-driven Stage increase is coarser than a category-local Substage^3 movement. It can correspond 
to a new major developmental envelope in which entire lower category/dimensional rules are 
recontextualized. A Raeyol-driven Hyperstage jump is coarser still: it moves above the entire bounded 
four-coordinate architecture of the current Hyperstage. Possible phenotypes at higher Hyperstages may 
therefore involve rule-of-rule reorganizations, new cross-system compatibility structures, altered 
developmental trajectories, new repair/response modalities, or gross anatomical novelty; exact 
manifestations remain CI/ILL until causal maps are canonized. 
The male mirror applies to Archmanhood: low and high male addresses can vary from outwardly familiar to 
visibly extraordinary while preserving the same proof discipline. Higher address does not logically mean 
larger muscles, higher testosterone, taller stature, or any stereotyped male metric. 
463. Womanhood versus Archwomanhood; manhood versus Archmanhood 
Ordinary womanhood is not the zero point of Archwomanhood and ordinary manhood is not the zero point of 
Archmanhood. A girl/woman can undergo ordinary puberty, maturation, anatomy/physiology change, aging, 
and life-history development while all Archwomanhood coordinates remain fixed. Likewise a boy/man can 
develop ordinarily while Archmanhood is stagnant. Conversely, Archstage coordinates can change while 
some ordinary developmental variables are temporarily constant. 
The medical specialties therefore monitor both systems. A treatment that produces a desired Archstage 
trajectory but poor short-term comfort, undesirable timing relative to ordinary puberty/maturation, or 
mismatch with the patient's goals can be clinically suboptimal even if HGL whole-form quality is rising. That 
is a wellbeing/trajectory statement, not a contradiction of the quality theorem. 
464. Final synthesis of the endocrine-medical revision 
The current HGL architecture now has an explicit outer control hierarchy. The earlier 
SIH/PSIH/LSCC/PH/UD/quality system defines the constitutive logical nature of Archstates; the 
five-coordinate endocrine envelope defines how the fictional organism is driven through those states. 
Hormone amount determines when a hormone-driven jump occurs. Receptor sensitivity determines how 
large the corresponding jump is. Four intra-Hyperstage coordinates are Hyperomega-bounded; Hyperstage 
has no final ceiling. Medication can independently tune amount and sensitivity, so pacing and jump 
magnitude become separable clinical variables. Archgynocology and Archandrocology exist because the 
medically optimal trajectory is an individualized multi-axis control problem involving ordinary development, 
phenotype, adaptation, psychology, relationships, behaviour, preferences, and long-term wellbeing—not 
merely the maximization of Archstage.
