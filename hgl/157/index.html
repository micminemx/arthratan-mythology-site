F.1 A five-minute lay explanation 
Female and Male are not treated as volume knobs. Suprafemale and Supramale are recursive biological 
states. A compound condition inside PA creates an irreducible developmental route. A higher stage then 
keeps the previous stage through IH and also inherits the logical non-state of the opposite-side previous 
stage. That inheritance history can keep getting deeper forever in the finite sequence. The body can still 
mature normally while staying at one stage. 
F.2 A five-minute logician explanation 
HGL is a typed, non-homomorphic causal modal-like system with PA, IH, PH, UD/FD, SIH, LSCC, and 
Gamma as distinct operator families. PA accepts inner condition formulas but does not preserve 
conjunction/disjunction structure and is not generally monotone. IH accepts typed inheritable sources, 
projects to its operand, does not admit converse projection, and preserves source/depth as intensional 
identity. PH positively asserts complete whole-profile possession; NOT PH explicitly denies that possession 
and is not PH(NOT source). Current SF/SM recursion combines same-side IH, opposite structured-nonstate 
IH, same-side PH, opposite NOT-PH, same-side UD, and same-side NOT-FD. Female/Male category typing 
remains distinct from every one of these operators. 
F.3 A five-minute scientist explanation 
PA represents causal attribution of necessarily developable anatomy/physiology. IH represents retained 
developmental provenance. PH represents irreducible possession of a whole HGL phenotype-profile. Stage 
is therefore a structured causal/phenotypic history, not merely a label. To predict concrete phenotype, identify 
the stage’s PH nodes and profile map, active PA modes, IH provenance, maturity, environment, and history. 
Deduce profile membership when specified; do not invent concrete anatomy merely from a stage number. 
F.4 A five-minute AI explanation 
Use a typed AST. Keep PA, IH, PH, UD, and FD as distinct node types. Do not move NOT through PA, IH, 
PH, UD, or FD without an explicit rule. Do not distribute PA/IH/PH/UD/FD. Preserve IH provenance and PA 
irreducibility. Canonicalize SF1/SM1 as PA-only bases, SF2/SM2 as eight-conjunct bases, and Stage 3+ as 
six-conjunct successors. Track whole-form quality Q and magnitude K separately from category membership 
and from local phenotype measurements. Proof-trace every D/HI/CD/CI conclusion.
