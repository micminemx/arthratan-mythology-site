P.2b PH tests 
•​
Keep PH(NOT S) distinct from NOT PH(S). 
•​
Do not distribute PH over AND or OR. 
•​
Keep PH distinct from PA and IH. 
•​
A concrete trait is deducible from PH only if profile membership or a causal consequence is 
specified. 
•​
Do not define a stage using PH of that same stage; use stage-lagged lower-state PH sources. 
P.3 Stage-definition tests 
•​
SF1 exact: SF1 = PA(x AND NOT y). 
•​
SM1 exact: SM1 = PA(NOT x AND y). 
•​
SF2 exact: eight conjuncts — 2 IH + 1 PH + 1 NOT-PH + 1 UD + 2 standalone PA + 1 NOT-FD. 
•​
SM2 exact: eight conjuncts — 2 IH + 1 PH + 1 NOT-PH + 1 UD + 2 standalone PA + 1 NOT-FD. 
•​
Higher recursion begins with construction of Stage 3 from Stage 2.
