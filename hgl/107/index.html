198. Three different sources of phenotype novelty 
HGL should therefore distinguish at least: 
1.​
Interstage Archwomanhood/Archmanhood novelty — new PH sources introduced by moving to a 
higher order. 
2.​
Intrastage maturation of existing Archstage phenotypes — fuller expression of phenotype 
architecture already associated with the static stage. 
3.​
Ordinary womanhood/manhood phenotype emergence in a static Archstage context — new 
sex-developmental traits that arise without a stage increase. 
A fourth category can be added when needed: 
4.​
Interaction emergence — phenotype generated by interaction between static Archstage phenotype 
architecture and ordinary woman/man development. 
These categories can overlap causally but must not be conceptually collapsed.
