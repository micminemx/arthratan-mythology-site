411. Causal inferences and phenotype possibilities: womanhood versus Archwomanhood 
Ordinary womanhood remains capable of puberty, breast/reproductive maturation, pelvic/skeletal 
development, endocrine change, skin/hair change, tissue maturation, aging, repair, and individual variation 
without any Archwomanhood address. Archwomanhood adds the fictional HGL 
constructor/provenance/quality/metaexpression architecture. Therefore an Archfemale can remain at a fixed 
Archaddress and continue ordinary womanhood maturation; conversely, an Archaddress can advance 
without forcing one stereotypical visible change. 
ILL/CI low Supra: externally ordinary-looking female anatomy could be generated under a new 
whole-profile/provenance relation whose significant quality effect lies chiefly in tissue integration or 
physiological reliability. ILL/CI high Supra: a transordinal closure could reorganize many lower profile 
constraints while still sharing gross morphology with a much lower stage. Supra alone guarantees same-side 
NOT-FD at canonical successor/limit roles. 
412. Phenotype possibilities: Hyper active-coordinate progression 
Within one fixed Hyper dimensional stratum, coordinate alpha and coordinate alpha+1 are now genuinely 
generated states rather than merely declared points. A possible HF<1>[2,Omega] phenotype may differ from 
HF<1>[1,Omega] through a new microanatomical interface, a change in repair topology, a new whole-body 
compatibility relation, a changed developmental response to hormones, or a visible gross-anatomical 
reorganization. None of those exact traits is logically forced. What is forced is the five-role predecessor 
topology and, through CUQB, Significant Quality Increase when its premises hold. 
At HFLim(1,omega), a possible phenotype may depend on interactions among arbitrarily many finite 
Hyper-coordinate predecessor contributions. It could be visually subtle because the emergent difference lies 
in repair, resilience, timing, or latent counterfactual response; or visibly dramatic if a causal map makes 
closure-level organization manifest in gross anatomy. The limit itself is not defined by “looking infinite.” 
413. Phenotype possibilities: Hyper dimensional-order ascent 
HF2 remains much more than the second active coordinate of HF1. HF2 superinherits the entire HF1 axis 
and corresponding structured non-HM1 axis, positively possesses the HF1 whole profile, excludes the HM1 
whole profile, and treats HF1 as UD. A possible phenotype consequence is a new rule governing how an 
entire family of HF1-coordinate phenotypes can be jointly coordinated rather than one more local trait. This is 
why dimensional order is a higher structural operation than coordinate movement. 
414. Phenotype possibilities: Ultra and Apex active axes 
Ultra and Apex coordinate steps use three-role immediate-predecessor constructors. Their possible 
phenotypes can therefore be dominated by inherited source geometry and whole-form improvement without 
the Hyper-specific requirement to add positive PH-own and opposite NOT-PH at every coordinate. A UF 
coordinate limit may plausibly improve global compatibility among lower Ultra states; an AF coordinate limit 
may plausibly reorganize response/repair/developmental relations inherited from the entire preceding prefix. 
Exact anatomy remains CI/ILL until mapped.
