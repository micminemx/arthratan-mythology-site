293. Limitstage phenotype emergence versus mere accumulation 
A limit stage should not be imagined as a warehouse holding every earlier phenotype unchanged. The 
closure supplies source structures. A nonlinear phenotype map can generate properties of the closure 
organization itself. The emergent trait E may depend on the co-presence, ordering, source depth, or 
compatibility relation among an unbounded family of predecessors and fail to exist at every individual 
predecessor. 
Formally, Profile(LimF(lambda)) need not equal Union_{beta<lambda} Profile(SF^beta). PH-9 guarantees 
predecessor-profile possession; it does not prohibit a genuinely new whole-limit profile. This is the limit 
analogue of the earlier whole-profile nonreduction principle. 
294. Major-stage limits and nested closure scales 
There are several kinds of limit in HGL and they must not be collapsed. omega_sub is an ordinary 
local-substage limit. Omega_H is the HGL minor-hypercategory boundary with a much larger stipulated initial 
segment. omega_cat is the limit of the countable minor-category index inside one major stage. A major-stage 
boundary is a higher structural transition. LSCC governs canonical local ordinal limits; hypercategory and 
major-stage boundary rules can wrap or reinterpret completed closure according to their separately stated 
transition axioms. 
A future extension may define MajorLimitClosure over an entire family of minor-category closures. Current 
HGL already has a major-stage envelope and boundary bridge, so the safe present rule is: do not infer a 
major-limit constructor beyond what is explicitly stipulated. The verifier marks such claims OPEN unless a 
constructor is declared. 
295. Crosspattern and nullpattern reading of limit biology 
A limit state creates a powerful crosspattern: the presence of every same-side ancestral source co-occurs 
with the structured absence/nonstate of every opposite-side ancestral source, and these are jointly carried by 
IH/PH/UD while own-side flawhood is systematically absent. The recurring absence of own-side FD across 
the entire predecessor domain is a nullpattern. The co-occurrence of those nonflaw absences with positive 
antithetical UD presences is a limit-level metacrosspattern. 
This is analytically richer than saying “many stages accumulated.” The informational object is the relation 
among what is present, what is structurally non-opposite, what is explicitly nonflawed, and which source 
provenance is preserved across an unbounded domain. 
296. AI proof protocol for limit and transordinal claims 
Step 1: type the ordinal. Decide whether alpha is zero/base, successor, or limit and whether it lies inside a 
local minor category or denotes a category/major boundary. 
Step 2: construct the state with the correct constructor. Use SF1/SM1 base, special Stage 2, six-conjunct 
ordinal-general successor, or LSCC limit constructor. 
Step 3: derive only explicit operator consequences. Apply conjunction elimination, closure membership, IH 
projection, profile membership, and named bridge axioms. 
Step 4: if reasoning about quality, separate mu from delta and separate address cardinality from quality 
magnitude. 
Step 5: if reasoning at a limit, invoke LQSA only after LSCC has constructed the state. 
Step 6: if reasoning about an exact phenotype, require PH profile membership or an explicit causal law. 
Step 7: tag the result D, HI, CD, CI, ILL, or OPEN and record the exact rule chain.
