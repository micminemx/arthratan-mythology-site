61. Three model worlds for the same high stage 
Consider SF10. 
World A: the phenotype map is mostly microscopic. Stage 10 creates new cell regulation and tissue 
microarchitecture while external appearance changes little. 
World B: the map is network-physiological. Stage 10 chiefly changes timing, coordination, repair, and 
homeostasis among existing structures. 
World C: the map contains strong macro-level interaction terms. Stage 10 produces new visible anatomy 
because earlier inherited microstructures interact with maturity-dependent growth.

differ. This proves that explicit PH profile possession does not automatically determine every concrete trait 
outside the stipulated profile contents.
