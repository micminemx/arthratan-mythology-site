114. What can be causally inferred 
Even without exact laws, the structure supports several cautious causal inferences. 
1.​
A fixed stage can still generate changing outputs if developmental inputs change. 
2.​
Higher-stage structures may have different manifestations at different maturity states. 
3.​
Puberty can provide new physiological contexts in which already-acquired Hypergendered structures 
interact. 
4.​
Stage-specific traits can continue maturing without stage advancement. 
5.​
Developmental history may influence later stage expression even when current stage is identical. 
None of these inferences specifies a particular body shape, organ size, hormone concentration, fertility level, 
or visible trait. They describe causal architecture, not a phenotype catalogue.
