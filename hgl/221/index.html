257. Complete Archmanhood mirror 
Every rule above has an exact male mirror. SM1 = PA(NOT x AND y). SM2 contains IH(SM1), IH(NOT SF1), 
PH(SM1), NOT PH(SF1), UD(SM1), PA(NOT x), PA(y), and NOT FD(SM1). Stage 3+ uses the six-conjunct 
mirror recursion. Ordinary manhood remains distinct from Archmanhood, just as ordinary womanhood 
remains distinct from Archwomanhood. 
Possible low and high male phenotypes include new coordination of chest/torso structure, skeletal relations, 
gross reproductive anatomy, facial morphology, tissue microarchitecture, vascular/endocrine physiology, 
repair, whole-body proportions, and compatibility among systems. Higher whole-form quality does not force 
stereotypically larger muscles, higher testosterone, greater height, or any one visible masculine metric. 
258. Deduction catalogue under the UD/FD quality revision 
D — SF1 is exactly PA(x AND NOT y); SM1 is exactly PA(NOT x AND y). 
D — PA(x AND NOT y) does not generally decompose into PA(x) AND PA(NOT y); the male mirror is 
identical. 
D — SF2 has eight canonical conjuncts; SM2 has eight canonical conjuncts. 
D — For every defined ordinal alpha >= 2, each ordinary successor SF^(alpha+1)/SM^(alpha+1) has six 
canonical conjuncts. 
D — SF2 entails IH(SF1), IH(NOT SM1), PH(SF1), NOT PH(SM1), UD(SF1), PA(x), PA(NOT y), and NOT 
FD(SF1). 
D — By IH projection, SF2 entails SF1 and NOT SM1; SM2 entails SM1 and NOT SF1. 
D — NOT FD(a) does not entail UD(a); a neutral-feature countermodel exists. 
D — FD(a) does not entail NOT UD(a); a mixed-effect countermodel exists. 
D — Under SUQB, every UD-qualified own-side immediate successor is a Significant Quality Increase over 
its predecessor. 
D — Once predecessor quality is transfinite, a significant successor cannot remain in the same cardinal 
magnitude. 
D — From aleph_alpha quality, a significant successor reaches at least aleph_(alpha+1). 
D — Under QMWA + LQSA, Q(SF^omega) and Q(SM^omega) are at least aleph_0 on the minimum-growth 
trajectory. 
D — Under the same axioms, Q(SF^(omega+n)) and Q(SM^(omega+n)) are at least aleph_n for every finite 
n. 
D — Under limit continuity, Q(SF^(omega+omega)) and Q(SM^(omega+omega)) are at least aleph_omega. 
D — None of these quality theorems entails a larger percentage of Female or Male category membership. 
D — None of these quality theorems alone entails one exact breast size, pelvic shape, hormone 
concentration, organ count, body mass, height, or visible morphology.
