Q17. Does macro manifestation weaken the hidden-dimension thesis? No. It adds a visible projection to a 
rule-space that can still contain vast hidden dimensions. 
Q18. Does the male side work differently? No at the logical level; it is a symmetric Archmanhood rule-system 
unless a specific biological law creates asymmetry. 
Q19. Is a stage meta-phenotype the same as PH(SF^n)? No. PH concerns possession of the stage 
phenotype-profile; Γ governs the rule-space of actual phenotype expression. 
Q20. Does this require nested PA? No. PA remains unnested; Γ and modal semantics handle 
metaexpression separately.

263. Compact authoritative statement 
Every Archwomanhood and Archmanhood stage is a global biological metaexpression regime, not 
merely a hidden or microscopic state. The regime governs the rules and constraints of how 
female-typed or male-typed physical phenotypes can, must, cannot, and conditionally can express 
throughout the body. Because gross anatomy and outward appearance are phenotype domains, 
every valid Archstage necessarily has at least some stage-governed macroscopic anatomical and 
outwardly apparent realization. This requirement does not make every stage trait visible, does not 
make every stage visually unique, and does not make higher stage a scalar exaggeration. A static 
stage can continue producing changing visible womanhood/manhood outputs as ordinary 
development changes the parameters passed through the unchanged Γ.
