184. Hyperomega saturation and strict category-lift transitions 
SF^Omega_H != HF1; rather, Completed(SF-axis) ->_SIH HF1. 
SM^Omega_H != HM1; rather, Completed(SM-axis) ->_SIH HM1. 
HF-dimensional saturation != UF1; UF1 is SIH_dim(HF-domain) AND SIH_dim(NOT HM-domain). 
HM-dimensional saturation != UM1; UM1 is the exact mirror. 
UF-dimensional saturation != AF1; AF1 is SIH_dim(UF-domain) AND SIH_dim(NOT UM-domain). 
UM-dimensional saturation != AM1; AM1 is the exact mirror. 
The inequality/nonidentity is essential. If the completed source domain were identical to the state that 
superinherits it, the definition could create constitutive selfinheritance. HGL therefore adopts Strict 
Category-Lift Nonidentity: a category seed is built from the completed lower domain but is not that 
lower-domain terminal object. 
The transition is analogous not to one instant having two names, but to a new coordinate system being 
constructed from a fully completed lower coordinate space. The source remains recoverable through SIH 
provenance; it is not identified with the target.
