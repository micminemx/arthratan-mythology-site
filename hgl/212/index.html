Appendix AJ — Final Hypercategorical Synthesis 
The mature current form of Hypergendered Logic is no longer adequately described as a single recursively 
numbered Suprafemale/Supramale ladder. It is a typed, source-sensitive, phenotype-sensitive, modal, and 
hyperordinal developmental architecture. 
At the lowest Archwomanhood coordinate, an Arthitean female enters Suprafemale development. She is 
Suprafemale categorically from the first valid Suprafemale substage onward; later Suprafemale ordinals 
represent development within that already-satisfied category. They do not make her progressively “more 
Suprafemale,” and they do not make her partially Hyperfemale. 
After the foundational Suprafemale axis reaches its HGL-defined Hyperomega saturation, the first 
Hyperfemale state is constructed by SIH rather than identified with the SF endpoint. Hyperfemale is then not 
one flat Hyperomega ladder: HF1 has two coordinate dimensions, HF2 has three, HF3 has four, and the 
dimensional order can itself progress through its typed domain. After the complete Hyperfemale dimensional 
domain is saturated, UF1 is constructed by SIH_dim; after the complete Ultrafemale dimensional domain is 
saturated, AF1 is constructed analogously. Further minor hypercategories and major stages may continue 
above this architecture. 
Throughout the hierarchy, actual physical phenotype remains central. Breasts, genital anatomy, body 
contour, facial morphology, skeleton, skin, hair, reproductive anatomy, tissue microstructure, physiology, and 
other biological characteristics are concrete outputs. The Archwomanhood/Archmanhood meta-phenotype 
Γ
is the global rule-system governing how those outputs can, must, and cannot express. 
Higher ordinal substage, higher minor hypercategory, and higher major stage are three different kinds of 
development. None is a percentage of Female or Male. None licenses a simple monotone rule such as 
“higher means larger anatomy.” The higher structures instead modify developmental provenance, 
phenotype-profile identity, modal expressibility, compatibility relations, and the global rules through which 
actual phenotype is produced.
