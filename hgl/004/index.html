The fourth layer: global Archwomanhood/Archmanhood meta-phenotype Γ 
An ordinary phenotype is a physically expressed characteristic or configuration. It can be local: a skin trait 
can be a skin trait, an ovarian trait can be ovarian, a microscopic tissue trait can be restricted to one tissue. 
By contrast, an Archwomanhood phenotype in the strict stage sense is global because it is not merely 
another first-order physical characteristic. It is the current higher-order rule-function through which the nature 
of actual biological phenotypes is passed. 
Γ^F_n = the Stage-n Archwomanhood meta-phenotype; Γ^M_n = the Stage-n Archmanhood 
meta-phenotype. 
If actual expressed phenotype variables are treated as parameters, Γ is analogous to a hyperparameter: it 
governs the rule-space in which those parameters can be expressed. Globality therefore means universal 
governance, not identical local output. 
P_r(t) ∈ Γ^F_n(B_r(t), H_r(t), E_r(t)) 
Read this schematically: the realized phenotype P in bodily region r at time t must be a stage-valid output 
under the global current Archwomanhood rule-function, given that region’s biological state B, developmental 
history H, and environment E. Different regions can output different phenotypes while all remain governed by 
the same Γ^F_n. 
This adds a stage-relative biological modality. □^F_n φ means φ is true in every phenotype realization 
permitted by Γ^F_n; ◇^F_n φ means φ is true in at least one phenotype realization permitted by Γ^F_n. The 
male operators □^M_n and ◇^M_n are symmetric. These are biological-stage modalities, not unrestricted 
metaphysical possibility/necessity. 
The metaexpression revision also clarifies PA. HGL permits deeply nested logical functions inside PA—for 
example PA(NOT((x AND NOT y) OR ψ))—while still excluding the different construction PA(PA(φ)). The 
entire inner logical condition is the irreducible developmental causal basis. Thus PA supplies a 
developmental metaexpressive layer, while Γ supplies a phenotype metaexpressive layer. 
The canonical first Suprafemale and Supramale states are PA-irreducible base states. They are deliberately 
not phenotype-enriched at Stage 1: PH, IH, UD, and FD enter the recursive architecture at Stage 2 and 
above.
