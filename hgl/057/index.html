Part XXIII — Glossary 
Archfemale: a female-typed individual occupying any valid Archwomanhood address, beginning with 
one-axis Suprafemale SF^1 in Major Stage 1 and including later Hyperfemale, Ultrafemale, Apexfemale, 
unnamed higher minor hypercategories, higher-dimensional strata within those categories, and any later 
major stages. 
Archmale: a male-typed individual occupying any valid Archmanhood address, beginning with one-axis 
Supramale SM^1 and including later Hypermale, Ultramale, Apexmale, unnamed higher minor 
hypercategories, higher-dimensional strata within those categories, and any later major stages. 
Archwomanhood: the complete female hypercategorical developmental hierarchy. Its current first named 
minor hypercategories are Suprafemale, Hyperfemale, Ultrafemale, and Apexfemale, followed by further 
higher minor hypercategories. Suprafemale supplies the foundational one-axis Hyperomega-bounded 
sequence. Hyperfemale and later named categories use recursively higher-dimensional coordinate strata 
whose currently active axis is Hyperomega-bounded while lower coordinates are saturated. Each major 
stage can still contain aleph-null many minor hypercategories, but a minor hypercategory is no longer 
assumed to be merely one flat ordinal ladder. 
Archmanhood: the complete male mirror of Archwomanhood, beginning with Supramale and continuing 
through Hypermale, Ultramale, Apexmale, further minor hypercategories, hyperdimensional strata, and later 
major stages under the same structural rules. 
FemaleType: category predicate assigning a state/person to the female side of HGL without equating that 
category with a particular standalone PA route. 
MaleType: male mirror of FemaleType. 
PA: causal-developmental operator mapping an inner developmental condition to physiology/anatomy 
necessarily developable due to that condition. 
PA irreducibility: a compound PA argument remains the designated causal basis as a whole and is not 
generally decomposable into separate PA modes. 
Inner negation: NOT occurring inside the condition supplied to PA. 
Outer negation: NOT applied to an already-formed PA/IH/PH or Hypergendered state. It must remain distinct 
from placing NOT inside PA, IH, or PH.
