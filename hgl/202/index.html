219. Logical inferences that are strong but not bare theorems 
The following are HGL logical/structural inferences rather than exact phenotype deductions. 
HI1. Later minor hypercategories plausibly support a richer rule-relationship space because they inherit 
completed prior categories and add a distinct category-level metaexpressive regime. 
HI2. A later major stage plausibly supports a higher-order rule architecture than an earlier major stage 
because the major-stage envelope itself changes. 
HI3. Greater hypercategorical depth plausibly increases the number of ways macroscopic, microscopic, 
physiological, and developmental constraints can interact. 
HI4. Phenotype prediction can become harder at higher addresses while category classification remains 
exact. 
HI5. The visible difference between two adjacent addresses need not increase monotonically with ordinal 
rank. Some transitions may be visually dramatic; others may be dominated by counterfactual/modal 
changes.
