251. Causal inferences about visible expression 
CI1. A stage transition that materially changes Γ is plausibly capable of changing outward appearance 
because outward phenotype variables are inside Γ’s governed domain. 
CI2. Continued puberty under static Γ is plausibly capable of producing new visible phenotypes without stage 
advancement. 
CI3. Deep microscopic changes can contribute causally to later gross anatomy, so high-stage visible traits 
may have recursively deep invisible prerequisites. 
CI4. A high-order macro trait may be emergent from multiple individually non-diagnostic lower-level rules 
rather than from one simple stage-specific gene-like switch. 
CI5. Visual expression can become more coordinated across regions at higher stages if Γ contains stronger 
cross-region constraints, but that coordination is not deduced without a law. 
252. Worked Archfemale examples across scale and stage 
F-1 Ordinary womanhood 
A woman has physically expressed female anatomy across ordinary microscopic, physiological, 
gross-anatomical, and outward domains. No Archwomanhood conclusion follows merely from those traits. 
F-2 SF1 
SF1 adds a global Stage-1 Archwomanhood metaexpression regime. At least some gross and outward 
female phenotype is stage-governed, while many ordinary female traits can remain familiar. Exact anatomy 
remains underdetermined. 
F-3 SF2 
SF2 inherits SF1 structure and carries the revised PA/IH/PH architecture. Its Γ can coordinate multiple 
female phenotype routes. A possible macro effect is a stage-specific compatibility relation among breast, 
skeletal, skin, and reproductive phenotype variables; the exact relation is illustrative. 
F-4 SF5 plateau during puberty 
Γ^F_5 is static. Pubertal inputs change. Breast maturation, body-contour change, skin/hair change, skeletal 
maturation, and reproductive maturation can continue as womanhood outputs under Γ^F_5 without SF6. 
F-5 SF10 
The stage necessarily has some macro/outward expression, but much additional Stage-10 novelty can be 
microscopic, physiological, or counterfactual. SF10 need not advertise every higher-order difference visibly. 
F-6 SF20 
A possible phenotype-space includes macroscopic female configurations whose legality depends on several 
inherited stage constraints simultaneously. The result may be visibly distinctive without being a simple 
enlargement or exaggeration. 
F-7 SF50 
A possible Stage-50 outward phenotype can remain recognizably female while its exact relations among 
anatomy, proportion, timing, and systemic physiology require Γ^F_50 to explain. This is CI/example, not an 
exact prediction.
