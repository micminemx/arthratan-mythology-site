180. Final statement of the revised insight 
Hypergendered Logic now has a clear answer to the question “what grows at higher order?” The answer is 
not PA nesting, not chromosome percentage, not repeated bare negation, and not arbitrary new labels. What 
grows is a source-preserving inheritance architecture whose own-side past and opposite-side logical 
non-state are recursively carried into each new stage. 
This produces a hierarchy that can be indefinitely deep in the finite sense while keeping the spoken biology 
coherent: 
An Archfemale at a higher stage inherits the complete developmental architecture of the prior 
Archwomanhood stage and inherits the structured non-state of the corresponding prior 
Archmanhood stage. An Archmale does the mirror image. The stage explicitly determines its PH 
phenotype-profiles, while the concrete contents/expression of those profiles and additional 
woman/man phenotypes depend on the fictional phenotype map, maturity, and causal realization; 
the female/male category direction remains fixed. 
Part XXIX — The PH Phenotype-Possession Revision: authoritative 
integration 
This part is the authoritative phenotype-layer expansion of HGL. It must be read together with the 
PA-Irreducibility and IH-Inheritance rules. Where an earlier phenotype discussion said merely that “stage 
logic does not determine phenotype,” the corrected statement is now more precise: 
A stage formula containing PH terms does determine that the corresponding irreducible HGL 
phenotype-profiles are possessed. What remains underdetermined until the profile map is 
supplied is the concrete anatomical/physiological content of those profiles and any additional 
non-stage phenotype produced by maturity, environment, history, or emergence. 
That distinction removes a false dilemma between “HGL says nothing about phenotype” and “HGL fixes 
every visible body trait.” The correct theory lies between them.
